111I am a service member summoned in a service-related case. What information does a lawyer need?
Direct answer

The nature of the act and referral authority, whether an Internal Security Forces Court or the ordinary criminal courts, are needed. Notify your ministry's legal department and prepare military orders, administrative investigation records and the incident's investigative-board report.

Legal reference

Internal Security Forces Criminal Procedure Law No. 17 of 2008 and the Military Penal Code.

Required documents

The official summons, commencement-of-service order and the ministry's final investigative-board report.

112My case concerns absence from service. Which facts and dates need review?
Direct answer

Calculate the absence in days and hours. Short absence attracts disciplinary punishment; absence beyond the statutory period is classified as desertion and referred to an Internal Security Forces Court. Lawful excuses such as medical incapacity or force majeure must be established.

Legal reference

Articles 33–38 of Internal Security Forces Penal Law No. 14 of 2008 and the service law.

Required documents

The administrative absence order, military medical reports from official committees and commencement or return-to-duty letters.

113I received a disciplinary penalty in service. What determines whether I can challenge it?
Direct answer

Pursue the hierarchical grievance before the superior commander or competent minister within the military deadlines. Where the decision includes dismissal, expulsion or demotion, challenge before the Internal Security Forces Court of Cassation or Military Court of Cassation for procedural illegality.

Legal reference

Internal Security Forces Law No. 18 of 2011 and Internal Security Forces Criminal Procedure Law No. 17 of 2008.

Required documents

The penalty order, military service record and a legally reasoned challenge.

114My case relates to military or security work. What determines the competent court?
Direct answer

An offence committed in the course of and because of duty within military facilities falls within Internal Security Forces or military-court jurisdiction. An act outside duty or an assault on civilians falls within the ordinary criminal courts' jurisdiction.

Legal reference

Article 4 of Internal Security Forces Criminal Procedure Law No. 17 of 2008.

Required documents

The official duty order, incident report and operational orders in force at the time.

115A service-related judgment or decision was issued against me. Which documents and deadlines need immediate review?
Direct answer

The judgment or service date is critical. The time limit for cassation before the Internal Security Forces Court of Cassation is 30 days from pronouncement or notification. A cassation petition must identify the court's errors and seek quashing and retrial.

Legal reference

Articles 75 onwards of Internal Security Forces Criminal Procedure Law No. 17 of 2008.

Required documents

The original military decision, trial hearing records and the approved cassation petition.

161I was injured on military or security duty. Which documents are needed to assess injury and service entitlements?
Direct answer

Prepare an investigative-board report establishing that the injury occurred during and because of duty, and obtain referral to the Supreme Military Medical Committee to assess disability and determine entitlement to exceptional promotion, a wounded-person's award and a medical disability pension under the law.

Legal reference

The Military Service and Retirement Law and Internal Security Forces Service and Retirement Law No. 18 of 2011.

Required documents

The incident's investigative-board report, the military medical disability assessment and the duty commencement order.

162I missed service because of illness, detention or circumstances outside my control. Which evidence does a lawyer need?
Direct answer

Submit evidence of force majeure or a legally recognised unavoidable impediment as quickly as possible to prevent classification as desertion. Request an investigative board to establish the lawfulness of the absence and reverse salary suspension and criminal-accountability orders.

Legal reference

Articles 33 and 37 of the Internal Security Forces Penal Law and the Military Service Law.

Required documents

Official hospital reports, judicial detention orders where applicable and police reports concerning kidnapping or siege incidents.

163Military or official property entrusted to me is missing. Which records and facts require review?
Direct answer

Notify the immediate commander at once and prepare a loss report. Distinguish personal negligence, which entails financial liability at twice the value, from loss during battles or military operations, for which exemption may be granted by ministerial decision.

Legal reference

Law No. 31 of 2015 on Financial Liability of Employees and Persons Entrusted with Public Service, and military service laws.

Required documents

The original property receipt register, the field loss report and the military investigative-board report.
