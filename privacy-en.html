Privacy Policy and Legal Consultation Terms

Practice: Lawyer Saad Firas Amil Bar registration: 188572 Location: Baghdad, Republic of Iraq Last updated: November 2026

1. Legal protection of professional confidentiality

Lawyer Saad Firas Amil's practice observes the highest legally protected standards of professional confidentiality. All data, names, facts and attached documents provided by a person seeking advice are covered by the lawyer's binding professional duty under Article 42 of amended Iraqi Advocacy Law No. 173 of 1965, which expressly prohibits disclosure of any secret learned through professional work.

⚖️ Professional confidentiality is a binding legal duty: The lawyer may not disclose information or documents relating to clients or people seeking advice, under civil, disciplinary and criminal liability, except with their express permission or where required by law under a reasoned judicial order.

2. Data processing, hosting infrastructure and WhatsApp use

For technical and professional transparency, we explain the nature and limits of how your data is processed:

  • Data transmission: Form data is sent directly to the practice's administrative register over a standard encrypted web connection (HTTPS).
  • Hosting and record management: The website is hosted on Hostinger servers, with direct browsing of administrative data folders blocked by .htaccess rules. Consultation requests are accessible only to lawyer Saad Firas Amil and direct support staff bound by professional confidentiality.
  • No storage in the visitor's browser: The website is configured not to save consultation facts or transfer receipt images in your browser's Local Storage, protecting your privacy against local retrieval.
  • Limited WhatsApp messages: When you confirm an appointment, the generated link contains only the request number, name and a request to confirm the appointment. No case facts or opposing parties' names are included in the WhatsApp link. Conversations inside the application are subject to Meta's encryption policy.
  • No data sharing: Data is not shared with or sold to commercial or advertising third parties. Its use is limited to providing the requested legal consultation.

3. Data retention, preserving financial credit and deletion

  • Separating the case file from the financial record: The policy strictly separates two types of data to protect client confidentiality while preserving the client's full financial entitlement:
    • A. Case file, facts and attached documents: These are retained for 90 days only from submission, to organise appointments and follow up recommendations. They are then automatically and permanently deleted from active servers unless a formal case file is opened and the lawyer is retained. Backups are rotated and destroyed within a maximum of 30 days.
    • B. Limited financial credit record, preserving entitlement for one full year: To establish the client's right to use the amount paid for one full calendar year (365 days from the transfer date), a limited accounting record is kept entirely separate from the dispute facts. It contains only the official Docket ID, applicant's name, masked telephone number, transfer date and amount, and the explicit credit expiry date after 365 days. Case facts and documents are therefore erased after day 90 for confidentiality, while financial credit remains valid and usable under the file number until the year ends.
  • Right to request immediate deletion and WhatsApp limitations: You may request immediate deletion of your data and documents at any time without waiting for the retention period to end. This includes deletion of the active administrative record and all attachments from the practice's servers, with confirmation within 24 hours, as well as deleting the conversation locally. Deletion from the servers of Meta, which operates WhatsApp, is governed by the application's technical features and policies, including the time-limited Delete for Everyone feature available to users.
  • How to request deletion: Send a message to the practice's direct WhatsApp number (+964 780 122 2119) or email (law@saadamil.com) including the Docket ID and a deletion request.

4. Scope and limits of the legal consultation service

To avoid procedural misunderstanding and preserve your rights, the consultation scope is defined clearly:

  • Session duration: A focused, direct 30-minute telephone or video session with lawyer Saad Amil to discuss the case facts and procedural guidance. The lawyer separately reviews the file beforehand; this review time is not deducted from the oral session.
  • Document review: Review and examination of up to 3 essential official documents, with a reasonable maximum of 10 pages in total, to allow careful, detailed examination of the procedural basis.
  • Consultation outputs and opinion summary: Identifying the legal and procedural basis, explaining available routes and options, highlighting gaps and weaknesses, and providing a written summary of the main recommendations and options by WhatsApp or email within 24 hours after the session ends.
  • Notice that file review has begun: You receive an express message when the lawyer actually starts examining the file documents and assessing the facts, confirming the start of professional work and entitlement to fees. If the initial assessment shows that the file cannot be accepted, a legal impediment exists or there is a conflict of interest, the practice immediately declines and returns all fees paid without deduction.
  • Separate representation and petition drafting: The consultation covers advice and guidance. Detailed court petitions and submissions, or in-person representation before courts and government bodies, require a separate representation and legal engagement agreement.

5. Agreeing consultation fees

  • Consultation fees are determined through direct contact and agreement with the lawyer before any payment.
  • Within the Republic of Iraq: Agreed fees are paid through Super / Qi Card or approved wallets.
  • Expatriates and international companies: Agreed fees are paid directly to the official US JPMorgan Chase bank account by wire or ACH transfer.

6. Rescheduling, cancellation and refunds

  • Changing or postponing an appointment: You may request free postponement or rescheduling by notifying the practice by telephone or WhatsApp at least 3 hours before the agreed appointment.
  • Cancellation and full refund: If you request cancellation of the consultation before the lawyer begins examining the documents and facts, you are entitled to a full refund, less any actual bank transfer charges. The refund is approved once the original transfer is confirmed as settled and credited to the practice's bank account. After notice that actual file review has begun, fees cannot be refunded because professional services have started and legal time and effort have been allocated to examination.
  • Non-refundable circumstances and appointment follow-up: After the consultation takes place, or once notice of actual file review is sent, fees are earned for the professional legal time and effort spent and cannot be refunded. You retain the right to the session and its written summary. If you do not attend or respond to appointment coordination despite reminders within 30 days of payment, the file is temporarily closed administratively. The full fee credit remains in the limited accounting record for one calendar year (365 days from the payment date shown on the receipt). During that time you may resume the consultation and schedule the session without additional fees, even after the original facts and documents are deleted at 90 days under section 3.
  • If the practice cannot hold the session: If an urgent court matter prevents the scheduled session and the parties do not agree an alternative within 24 hours, a full fee refund is approved immediately without deduction, and this is confirmed to you.
  • Bank transfer processing timetable: Once the refund is approved, the bank transfer instruction is sent within 3–5 banking business days through the original payment method (Super / Qi Card / bank transfer), subject to standard bank clearing and verification procedures.
  • Scope of deletion: Deletion follows the scope, time periods, backup limits and WhatsApp limitations detailed in section 3 of this policy.

7. Working hours and contact

The practice's working hours are Saturday–Thursday, from 9:00 am to 6:00 pm (Baghdad time).
