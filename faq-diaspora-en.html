1I live outside Iraq and have a case in Baghdad. Must I return, or can I appoint a lawyer to handle it?
Direct answer

Your attendance is not required at all. Iraqi law allows you to appoint a lawyer under a general or special power of attorney authenticated by a consulate to represent you before all courts and authorities and pursue the case through a final judgment and its enforcement without travelling to Iraq.

Legal reference

Articles 51 and 52 of the amended Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

A consularly authenticated power of attorney for legal representation, plus a copy of the unified national ID card or a valid Iraqi passport.

2How do I arrange a power of attorney for a lawyer in Iraq while living abroad, and what information is needed?
Direct answer

Visit the Iraqi consulate or embassy in your country of residence to execute the power of attorney. Send the original to Baghdad for authentication by the Consular Department of the Iraqi Ministry of Foreign Affairs and official approval by the Iraqi Bar Association.

Legal reference

Notaries Public Law No. 33 of 1998 and Foreign Service Law No. 36 of 2024.

Required documents

The principal's and lawyer's full four-part names and surnames, ID or passport numbers, and a precise statement of the powers granted, including litigation, settlement, admission, release, arbitration and receipt of funds.

3Before appointing a lawyer from abroad, which documents and information should I send for a case assessment?
Direct answer

Provide copies of the principal documents supporting your claim and a precise chronology of events so the lawyer can assess the procedural position and prospects of litigation before starting and avoid dismissal on procedural grounds.

Legal reference

Article 7 of Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979: the burden of proof rests on the claimant.

Required documents

Copies of contracts, receipts, property title documents and court notices; an identity document or passport; and precise dates relating to the dispute.

4I have an old power of attorney executed outside Iraq. How can I tell whether it covers the action I need?
Direct answer

The wording is reviewed to establish whether the powers are comprehensive or restricted and whether the intended action, such as sale, mortgage or subdivision, is expressly covered. It is also necessary to confirm that the principal and agent are alive, that no formal revocation has been recorded with a notary public and that any specified term has not expired.

Legal reference

Articles 927–947 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The original power of attorney, foreign authentication endorsements and a notary public's confirmation that the authority remains valid.

5My father died leaving property in Iraq, and I live abroad. How do I begin dealing with the inheritance?
Direct answer

Start by obtaining a Sharia or civil inheritance certificate establishing the heirs and their shares. Then arrange an inspection, obtain property register extracts from the Real Estate Registration Department, including a recent Form 25 title extract, and proceed with an agreed division or a court action to terminate co-ownership.

Legal reference

Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959 and Real Estate Registration Law No. 43 of 1971.

Required documents

The original death certificate, a family civil-register extract from the 1957 records, the heirs' identity documents and the property and cadastral district numbers.

6One heir has taken over the family home. How can I claim my rights while outside Iraq?
Direct answer

Bring an action to prevent interference and compel delivery before the competent Court of First Instance. Also seek compensation based on the property's fair rental value for the entire period during which that heir alone used the property and excluded the other co-owners, as assessed by court-appointed experts.

Legal reference

Articles 1070 and 1073 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The inheritance certificate, a recent Form 25 property title extract and a power of attorney covering the proceedings and inspection.

7I want to sell my property in Baghdad while abroad. Which steps need a lawyer's review?
Direct answer

Execute a special power of attorney for sale at the embassy that accurately identifies the property and cadastral district. Have it authenticated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Bar Association, obtain a recent title extract and municipal and tax clearances, obtain security approval, and transfer ownership through the Real Estate Registration Department.

Legal reference

Real Estate Registration Law No. 43 of 1971 and the applicable financial and tax instructions.

Required documents

An authenticated special power of attorney for sale, the property's title document, municipal clearance and completion of the General Commission for Taxes procedures.

8I live abroad and want to buy property in Iraq. What should be checked before transferring money or signing?
Direct answer

Check a recent Form 25 property register extract with the Real Estate Registration Department to confirm that there is no attachment, mortgage, endowment or easement. Verify the engineering plan and the absence of violations, and avoid paying a deposit outside a formally documented contract.

Legal reference

Article 508 of the Civil Code and Real Estate Registration Law No. 43 of 1971.

Required documents

A recent title extract from the Real Estate Registration Department, confirmation of the extract's authenticity and the site cadastral map.

9Someone has encroached on my property in Iraq while I am away. How can I pursue the matter legally?
Direct answer

Bring an action to remove the encroachment and prevent interference before the competent Court of First Instance. Where there has been unlawful seizure, a criminal complaint may be made under Revolutionary Command Council Decision No. 154 of 2001, together with a claim for compensation and fair rental value.

Legal reference

Article 1070 of the Civil Code and amended Decision No. 154 of 2001 of the dissolved Revolutionary Command Council.

Required documents

The original title document, a municipal inspection report or urgent evidence-preservation report, and a power of attorney authorising the relevant procedures.

10I sent money to someone in Iraq to buy or invest for me, and they now deny the agreement. What evidence do I need?
Direct answer

Establish the transfer and receipt of the funds and the entrusted or investment purpose through correspondence and documents. Then bring a civil claim for repayment on the basis of unjust enrichment, or a criminal complaint for breach of trust if deception and appropriation are established.

Legal reference

Article 453 of the Penal Code and Articles 107 onwards of the Evidence Law.

Required documents

Bank or exchange-office transfer records, WhatsApp conversations or emails establishing the agreement, and a formal demand for repayment.

11Someone in Iraq owes me money, and I live abroad. How can I recover the debt?
Direct answer

Bring a debt claim before the Court of First Instance for the debtor's domicile, relying on written debt evidence or transfer records. If there is no written instrument, the dispute may be determined by requesting a decisive oath.

Legal reference

Articles 77 and 108 of Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979.

Required documents

The debt instrument or receipt, money-transfer statements, or a request for a decisive oath before the Court of First Instance judge.

12I have a foreign divorce judgment. What is needed to assess whether it can be registered or recognised in Iraq?
Direct answer

Bring proceedings before the competent Court of First Instance to have the foreign judgment declared enforceable. The judgment must be final, issued by a court with jurisdiction, and consistent with public order, Islamic Sharia principles and Iraqi personal status law.

Legal reference

The Foreign Judgments Enforcement Law No. 30 of 1928, currently in force.

Required documents

The original divorce judgment authenticated through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and embassy, a sworn legal translation and a certificate confirming that the judgment is final.

13I married outside Iraq. Which documents are needed to register the marriage in Iraq?
Direct answer

Have the foreign marriage contract authenticated by the Iraqi embassy and Iraqi Ministry of Foreign Affairs and officially translated. Then bring an action before the Personal Status Court to establish and authenticate the marriage concluded abroad, record it in the civil register and obtain an Iraqi marriage contract.

Legal reference

Article 10 of the amended Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959.

Required documents

The authenticated foreign marriage contract, both spouses' civil status or unified national ID cards, and the approved forensic medical examination.

14My child was born abroad. What procedures relate to registering the birth and obtaining Iraqi documents?
Direct answer

Have the foreign birth certificate authenticated by the consulate, obtain an Iraqi birth certificate through the consulate, and transfer the birth entry to the Civil Status and Passports Directorate records in Baghdad so a unified national ID card can be issued for the child.

Legal reference

Births and Deaths Registration Law No. 148 of 1971 and National ID Card Law No. 3 of 2016.

Required documents

The authenticated foreign birth record, the parents' authenticated Iraqi marriage contract, and the father's and mother's civil status or national ID cards.

15My children and I live abroad, and there is a custody or travel dispute. What information does a lawyer need?
Direct answer

Establish the children's nationality and habitual residence and whether there are earlier foreign court orders. Review the father's legal guardianship, the custodian's rights, the child's best interests and the court with territorial jurisdiction.

Legal reference

Article 57 of the amended Iraqi Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959.

Required documents

The children's birth certificates, any previous judgments or court orders, and residence documents for the country where they live.

16I want to return to Iraq but fear that a complaint or court action may be pending. Which official enquiry routes are available?
Direct answer

Appoint an Iraqi lawyer under a formal power of attorney to make enquiries with the Central Computer Section of the Criminal Evidence Directorate, the judicial police and the competent Investigation Courts to check accurately and safely for arrest warrants or wanted notices.

Legal reference

Articles 99 onwards of Criminal Procedure Law No. 23 of 1971.

Required documents

An authenticated power of attorney, the full four-part name and surname, mother's name, and date and place of birth for matching against the official criminal-record system.

17I received notice of an Iraqi court case while abroad. What should I check first?
Direct answer

Check the service date, hearing date, case number and court name, and verify that service abroad followed the required diplomatic channels. Appoint a lawyer promptly to avoid a judgment in your absence.

Legal reference

Articles 21–27 of the amended Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

A copy of the court notice, the mailing envelope or consular notification, and the precise date of actual receipt.

18A judgment was issued against me while abroad. What is needed to assess an objection or appeal?
Direct answer

Identify whether the judgment was issued in absentia or in the parties' presence, establish the date of formal service, and calculate the mandatory time limits for an objection to an in-absentia judgment, an appeal or cassation. This allows the challenge to be prepared before the judgment becomes final.

Legal reference

Articles 171, 177, 185 and 203 of Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

An officially certified copy of the judgment, a copy of the service file and the date of actual service to establish when the statutory period begins.

19I live abroad and want to establish a company or join a partnership in Iraq. What legal arrangements are needed?
Direct answer

Choose the appropriate legal form, usually a limited liability company, reserve the trade name, prepare and authenticate well-drafted incorporation documents, deposit the capital with an Iraqi bank, and complete the Companies Registration Department and Chamber of Commerce procedures.

Legal reference

The amended Iraqi Companies Law No. 21 of 1997.

Required documents

A consularly authenticated special power of attorney for company formation, copies of passports or national ID cards, a description of the company's activities and a lease for its premises.

20I need to use a document issued in another country before an Iraqi authority. What authentication requirements apply?
Direct answer

The document must follow the prescribed diplomatic authentication chain: the issuing country's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, then the Iraqi embassy in that country, then the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Baghdad, followed by a sworn legal translation through the Iraqi Translators Association.

Legal reference

Article 27 of Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979 and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs Law in force.

Required documents

The original document bearing the original consular stamps, together with the officially certified translation.

166My agent in Iraq used a power of attorney contrary to our agreement. Which documents are needed to assess the transactions?
Direct answer

Immediately revoke the power of attorney and remove the agent before a notary public. Seek invalidation of acts exceeding the authority, an accounting and repayment of misappropriated funds, and bring a criminal breach-of-trust complaint under Article 453 of the Penal Code.

Legal reference

Articles 927–947 of the Civil Code and Article 453 of Penal Code No. 111 of 1969.

Required documents

The power of attorney, instruments for the unauthorised contracts and transactions, and the notarial revocation notice.

167I live abroad and want to revoke my Iraqi agent's authority. How are revocation and notification arranged?
Direct answer

Execute an official revocation at the Iraqi embassy in your country of residence and authenticate it through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Baghdad. Lodge it with the notary public's office that issued the authority so revocation is entered in the original register and the Real Estate Registration Department and courts are notified, preventing subsequent acts from taking effect.

Legal reference

Articles 945 and 946 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The embassy-executed revocation, the revoked authority's number and date, and official notification served on the agent.

168My foreign passport name differs from Iraqi records. What is needed before a property or inheritance transaction?
Direct answer

Obtain a name-matching certificate and birth extract from the embassy or Ministry of Foreign Affairs, or seek correction and unification of the name or surname before the Court of First Instance or Civil Status Court to obtain an official identity-matching certificate before dealing with property or receiving an inheritance share.

Legal reference

The National ID Card Law, Civil Status Law and Civil Procedure Law.

Required documents

The foreign passport, Iraqi passport or old civil status ID, nationality certificate and consular name-matching confirmation.

169I have a foreign money judgment against someone with Iraqi assets. What determines enforceability in Iraq?
Direct answer

Seek recognition and enforceability before the Baghdad Court of First Instance in Karkh or Rusafa. The judgment must be final, issued by a court with jurisdiction, enforceable in its country and based on reciprocity. Then lodge it with the Enforcement Directorate to attach the debtor's Iraqi assets.

Legal reference

The Foreign Judgments Enforcement Law No. 30 of 1928, currently in force.

Required documents

The original foreign judgment, consularly authenticated and legally translated, and proof that it is final and conclusive.

170I am Iraqi and hold another nationality. What information is needed to assess its effect on a transaction in Iraq?
Direct answer

Iraqi law recognises dual nationality under Article 18 of the Constitution. Within Iraq, however, an Iraqi with another nationality is treated solely as an Iraqi citizen using Iraqi documents, is subject to Iraqi property, inheritance and litigation laws, and presents Iraqi identification for all transactions.

Legal reference

Article 18(Fourth) of the 2005 Constitution of the Republic of Iraq and Iraqi Nationality Law No. 26 of 2006.

Required documents

The unified national ID or Iraqi civil status card, Iraqi nationality certificate and Iraqi passport.
