Client guide to the Personal Status Court: a safety roadmap

Three essential procedural rules to protect your rights and legal position:

1. Staying marital obedience proceedings

If the husband brings a marital obedience claim, the lawyer formally requests a stay when a separation claim is filed, relying on Cassation Decision 894 so the marital dispute is resolved first.

2. An alternative where harm cannot be proved (Article 41)

If direct harm cannot be proved and the Article 40 claim is dismissed, the lawyer guides you towards separation for irreconcilable discord under Article 41, with marital entitlements settled according to the arbitrators' findings and the court's decision.

3. Custody serves the child's interests

We review the child's age, marriage documents and earlier decisions to identify the rules applicable to your file. The starting point is the child's best interests, without prejudicing either party's rights.

Personal status legal questions

Procedural answers based on Iraqi legislation in force and Federal Court of Cassation decisions:

Will a judge grant separation for harm if the wife cannot prove assault through witnesses?

If the wife cannot provide direct evidence of harm and her claim under Article 40(1) of the Personal Status Law is dismissed, the procedural alternative is separation for irreconcilable discord under Article 41 of amended Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959.

The court appoints two arbitrators from the parties' families to seek reconciliation. If reconciliation fails and discord persists, the judge orders separation in accordance with their findings and the law, without forcing the wife to continue the marriage.

Source: Personal Status Law Article 41 + Al-Wasit by adviser Farouq Abdullah Karim
How can a lawyer stay a marital obedience claim brought by a husband to pressure his wife?

Iraqi Federal Court of Cassation Decision No. 894 establishes a binding procedural rule: if the wife brings a separation claim against her husband, the Personal Status Court must stay the marital obedience proceedings until that claim is resolved and its judgment becomes final.

The lawyer therefore requests a stay to avoid a finding of marital disobedience during the dispute and directs the proceedings towards claiming her procedural rights before the competent court.

Source: Federal Court of Cassation Decision No. 894 / Personal Status / application of Civil Procedure Article 83
Must a wife legally live with her husband's family in the same house?

No. Iraqi law and settled cassation case law require the lawful marital home under Personal Status Law Article 26 to have independent facilities and entrances, no other residents or members of the husband's family, suitable furniture and a safe neighbourhood with suitable neighbours.

If the husband provides a room in his family's home or requires shared residence without her consent, the lawyer challenges the home's legal suitability and independence and seeks dismissal of the obedience claim. Its suitability remains subject to the judicial expert's report and the court's assessment.

Source: Personal Status Law Article 26 + Abd al-Rahman al-Allam's commentaries
When does a mother's custody end under Article 57 of the Personal Status Law?

We review the child's age, marriage documents and earlier decisions to identify the rules applicable to your file. Iraqi court rulings place the child's best interests at the centre of custody. Custody ends in specific, limited statutory circumstances:

1. The mother marries someone unrelated to the child, unless the court decides the child's best interests require otherwise.
2. Loss of mental capacity or serious neglect threatening the child's health or upbringing is established by recognised medical and judicial decisions.
3. The child reaches the statutory age for choosing before the competent personal status judge.

Source: Personal Status Law Article 57, its amendments and Federal Court of Cassation decisions
Can Iraqi courts award deferred dower valued in gold?

Yes. Cassation case law establishes that if the marriage contract records the dower in gold, for example 20 mithqals of 21-carat gold, the husband must deliver the gold itself or its current market value in Iraqi dinars at the time of the claim and enforcement.

The price at the time of marriage is disregarded to prevent substantial unfairness caused by currency inflation.

Source: Personal Status Law Article 19 + General Assembly decisions of the Federal Court of Cassation
How can a wife recover her listed matrimonial furniture in kind or in cash?

The listed furniture belongs exclusively to the wife. She may recover the items through a matrimonial furniture recovery claim before the Court of First Instance, or their cash value assessed by a judicial expert if destroyed or concealed.

A breach of trust complaint under Iraqi Penal Code Article 453 may also be brought if the husband disposes of the furniture or refuses to deliver it in bad faith, exposing him to imprisonment and an obligation to return it.

Source: Penal Code Article 453 + Civil Code Article 1165
What is the mandatory bequest under Article 74, and how do grandchildren inherit from their grandparent?

Article 74 of Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959 gives grandchildren of a son or daughter who died before the person leaving the estate a statutory entitlement called a mandatory bequest.

It equals the share their parent would have received if alive, up to one third of the estate. The mandatory bequest takes priority over the other heirs in the inheritance certificate and prevents orphans being deprived of their grandparents' estate.

Source: Personal Status Law Article 74, amended by Law No. 72 of 1979
