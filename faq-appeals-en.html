116A judgment was issued against me. What determines whether and how I can challenge it?
Direct answer

The court type, such as First Instance, Personal Status or Misdemeanour, the claim value and whether the judgment was issued in the parties' presence or in absentia determine the route. Routes include objection to an in-absentia judgment, appeal, cassation, correction of a cassation decision and retrial, subject to mandatory time limits.

Legal reference

Articles 168–220 of the amended Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

A certified judgment, its formal service date and hearing minutes.

117A judgment was issued in my absence. What does a lawyer need to assess an objection?
Direct answer

File an objection before the same court within ten days of formal service in civil and personal status matters. Legal acceptance suspends enforcement and reopens proceedings so all defences can be heard.

Legal reference

Articles 177–184 of the amended Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

The original in-absentia judgment, service document, objection petition and exculpatory and defence documents.

118I only learned of the case after judgment. What should be checked about service?
Direct answer

Review the service document, the process server's and mukhtar's endorsements and receipt signatures. Service at an incorrect address or through an unauthorised signature is invalid, invalidating the in-absentia judgment and reopening the legal challenge route without the time limit being lost.

Legal reference

Articles 21–27 of the amended Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

The court service file and evidence of the actual address differing from the notice, such as a residence card.

119I am told the judgment is final. What needs review before deciding whether any legal route remains?
Direct answer

Verify the service date and mandatory challenge periods. If the periods were missed, the judgment becomes final; the remaining routes are retrial where fraud or forgery emerges, or correction of the cassation decision under its conditions.

Legal reference

Articles 171, 196 and 219 of Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

The record confirming finality and examination of any decisive documents that were deliberately withheld.

120I obtained a new document or evidence after judgment. What determines whether it can be used?
Direct answer

Seek retrial before the court that issued the judgment, provided the document is decisive and the opponent prevented its production or it was unknown. Proceedings must be initiated within a mandatory 15 days from the official discovery date.

Legal reference

Article 196(3) of the amended Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

The decisive new document or evidence, proof of its discovery date and the previous judgment.

121I want to challenge a judgment but fear enforcement during the challenge. What determines whether a stay can be sought?
Direct answer

Cassation generally does not suspend enforcement except in property cases. The judgment debtor may seek a temporary stay from the Court of Cassation or enforcement officer, subject to bank or property security protecting the judgment creditor against harm.

Legal reference

Article 194 of the Civil Procedure Law and Article 53 of Iraqi Enforcement Law No. 45 of 1980.

Required documents

The filed cassation petition, a stay application and adequate solvent security.

122I think the appeal deadline passed. Which facts and dates should a lawyer review?
Direct answer

Check whether the final day fell on an official holiday or during force majeure, extending the deadline to the next official working day under Article 173 of the Civil Procedure Law. Also verify whether the judgment debtor was validly served in the first place.

Legal reference

Articles 172 and 173 of the amended Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

The official holiday calendar and evidence of curfews or force majeure coinciding with the deadline.

123Before asking a lawyer to assess an appeal, which judgment, hearing records and documents should I prepare?
Direct answer

Obtain a certified copy of the challenged judgment including its reasons and governing provisions, complete copies of the hearing minutes, and all pleadings, documents and technical reports exchanged before the first-instance court.

Legal reference

The Judicial Fees Law and Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

The original judgment, hearing minutes and reports of court-appointed experts.

181A court ordered me to pay compensation that I consider excessive. Can I challenge the judgment?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical answer: Yes, certainly; your right is protected by law. Procedure: Compensation judgments issued by a Court of First Instance may be appealed within 15 days from the day after service or public explanation of the judgment, then challenged by cassation within 30 days. Grounds: We focus on defects in the court expert's valuation, amounts exceeding the actual harm and disproportionality under Articles 207 and 208 of the Civil Code. Before the Court of Appeal, we firmly request a panel of three or five experts to reassess the harm and reduce the award to a realistic level.

182A property ownership or co-ownership termination judgment harmed me. How do I know whether I can challenge it?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical answer: Distinguish strictly between two types of judgment. Property ownership and substantive rights: Appeal to the Court of Appeal within 15 days, followed by cassation to the Federal Court of Cassation's Property Panel within 30 days. Termination of co-ownership or partition: These judgments are issued at final instance and cannot be appealed. The only route is cassation to the Court of Appeal acting in its cassation capacity within 30 days under Articles 31 and 204 of the Civil Procedure Law. Possible grounds: A grossly unfair valuation, proof that physical partition is possible without public auction, or failure to join a co-owner in the inheritance certificate.

183An eviction judgment was issued against me. What is needed to assess a challenge?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical answer: Residential eviction judgments under Property Rent Law No. 87 of 1979 may be challenged only by cassation before the Court of Appeal acting in its cassation capacity, within 30 days. Documents needed immediately: A copy of the eviction judgment. The lease and property title. The notarial notice and actual receipt date. Rent-payment receipts or court-deposit receipts, to refute non-payment where that is the eviction ground.

184A maintenance judgment was issued, and I disputed the amount from the outset. What is needed to assess cassation?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical answer: This is among the most critical Iraqi court deadlines. Cassation against Personal Status Court judgments is limited to ten days from explanation or service under Article 204 of the Civil Procedure Law. Route: The Court of Appeal acting in its cassation capacity. Assessment: Provide proof of actual income, including salary confirmation, documented debts and support for other children or parents, to challenge the researcher's assessment and establish a burden beyond your legally prescribed means. After expiry: If the judgment becomes final, we immediately bring a maintenance-reduction action under Article 28 of the Personal Status Law based on changed financial circumstances.

185I disagree with a child custody or contact decision. Which options need review?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical answer: Custody and contact cases turn on the child's best interests. Route: Cassation within ten days before the Court of Cassation or the Court of Appeal acting in its cassation capacity. Review includes: Custody: Article 57 conditions, the custodian's trustworthiness and conduct, social research and any psychiatric reports, and the mother's marriage to an unrelated man where this harms the child. Contact and outings: Challenge the time and place to seek a safe, comfortable setting and expanded contact, including overnight stays on holidays and festivals, to strengthen family ties.

186I dispute the facts or evidence underlying a divorce or judicial separation judgment. What is needed for review?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical answer: Judicial separation, divorce and marital resumption judgments are challenged before the Federal Court of Cassation's Personal Status Panel within a mandatory ten days under Article 204 of the Civil Procedure Law. Documents: The judgment and hearing minutes. The relatives' arbitrators' report under Articles 40 and 41 of the Personal Status Law. Witness statements to identify contradiction or hearsay. Review of the assigned share of fault, because errors in apportionment can deprive a spouse of deferred dower, compensation or household furniture.

187A Misdemeanour Court convicted me. What does a lawyer need to assess cassation?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical answer: Misdemeanour Court judgments imposing imprisonment from 24 hours to five years or fines may be challenged before the Court of Appeal acting in its cassation capacity within 30 days, starting the day after a judgment pronounced in the defendant's presence or service of an in-absentia judgment, under Article 249 of the Criminal Procedure Law. Review materials: The judgment and reasons. Trial minutes and statements of the accused, complainant and witnesses. Procedural defects, including invalid investigation or arrest, expiry through limitation or settlement, or insufficient evidence under Article 259, to seek cancellation of the charge and release.

188A family member received a Criminal Court judgment. What should we prepare for review?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical answer: Criminal Court judgments imposing a fixed prison term, life imprisonment or death concern liberty and a person's future. Cassation lies to the Federal Court of Cassation's Criminal Panel within 30 days under Article 249 of the Criminal Procedure Law. Prepare: The conviction and sentencing decisions. The complete investigation papers, witness statements and exhibit-seizure records. Identify overlooked substantive defences: confessions obtained through coercion or torture under Article 127 of the Criminal Procedure Law and Article 37 of the Constitution, and incorrect legal classification. Seek a lesser classification, for example from intentional murder under Article 406 to a fight under Article 405 or assault causing death under Article 410, to substantially reduce the sentence.

189I object to a decision during an investigation. What determines whether and where it can be challenged?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical answer: Determinative Investigation Court decisions, including detention, refusal of bail, rejection and closure with release under Article 130(B), or referral for trial, may be challenged by cassation. Competent court: Exclusively the Criminal Court acting in its cassation capacity under Article 265 of the Criminal Procedure Law. Period: 30 days from the investigation decision or service. Essential rule: Temporary preparatory decisions that do not determine the dispute are challenged only with the final decision determining the investigation.

190I won, but the other party challenged the judgment. What should I do on receiving their petition?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical answer: This is a highly important defence step and must not be ignored, to avoid being surprised by reversal of the judgment in your favour. Procedure: We prepare and file a response to the challenge within the statutory period. Cassation: Within ten days of service of the cassation petition under Article 207 of the Civil Procedure Law. Purpose: Refute each ground, demonstrate the court's correct application of law and evidence, establish the ineffectiveness of the opponent's arguments, and seek affirmation, dismissal of the challenge and an award of fees and costs against the opponent.

191The case returned to court after a challenge. How do we understand the new stage and arrange follow-up?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical answer: Return means the reviewing court quashed the judgment wholly or partly and remitted the case for further proceedings in accordance with its cassation directions. Follow-up: Read the cassation decision literally and carefully to identify whether it requires a new inspection, previously omitted witnesses or correction of a legal error. The trial court must follow the cassation directions contained in the quashing decision. We immediately seek restoration of the file to the proceedings, attend the scheduled hearing and rely on the directions to advance the case and obtain a final determination.

192The worker or employer disagrees with a labour entitlements judgment. What is needed for review?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical answer: Labour disputes are governed by Labour Law No. 37 of 2015 and fall within the Labour Court's jurisdiction. Route: Cassation to the Federal Court of Cassation's Labour Cases Panel within a mandatory 30 days from the day after service under Article 167 of the Labour Law. Review materials: The employment contract, proof of wages and service, termination committee decisions, calculations of end-of-service benefits and accumulated leave, and evidence establishing whether dismissal was arbitrary or legally justified.
