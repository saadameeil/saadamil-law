76My employer dismissed me, and I believe it was unjustified. What do I need to assess my rights?
Direct answer

Dismissal without a lawful reason is unfair dismissal, entitling the worker to bring a Labour Court claim within 30 days for notice pay, an end-of-service benefit and fair compensation for material and non-material harm caused by termination.

Legal reference

Articles 42–47 of Iraqi Labour Law No. 37 of 2015.

Required documents

The termination letter or proof of dismissal, the employment contract if available, evidence of the last salary received and the length of service.

77My company has not paid my wages. Which evidence is needed to claim them?
Direct answer

Establish continuous work and performance of duties through attendance records or administrative correspondence, and bring a Labour Court action to require payment of arrears, compensation and statutory late-payment interest.

Legal reference

Articles 53–58 of Iraqi Labour Law No. 37 of 2015.

Required documents

Bank statements, attendance cards, work messages or testimony from colleagues where records are unavailable.

78I work without a written contract. What can establish the employment relationship?
Direct answer

Iraqi legislation permits a worker to prove the employment relationship and its terms by all lawful means, including witnesses, a work ID card, WhatsApp conversations and financial transfers. The relationship is recognised and subject to the law in full.

Legal reference

Article 37(Third) of Iraqi Labour Law No. 37 of 2015.

Required documents

A work ID, bank statements showing regular transfers, WhatsApp messages and details of witnesses working at the establishment.

79My employment ended, and payments remain due. What information is needed to assess and calculate the claim?
Direct answer

Establish the precise length of service, basic salary and allowances, accumulated unused annual leave, and the end-of-service benefit of two weeks' wages per year for a worker not covered by retirement benefits.

Legal reference

Articles 45 and 75 of Iraqi Labour Law No. 37 of 2015.

Required documents

The starting and termination dates, the leave record and the final salary breakdown.

80I suffered an injury at work. Which documents does a lawyer need?
Direct answer

Obtain an official medical committee report establishing the percentage of physical disability and investigation reports from the police and occupational-safety officer establishing that the accident occurred at or because of work, to require the employer and social-security fund to pay compensation and disability benefits.

Legal reference

Labour Law No. 37 of 2015 and Workers' Retirement and Social Security Law No. 18 of 2023.

Required documents

The disability committee's medical report, the police accident report and immediate notification of the injury to the employer.

81I discovered that my employer did not register me for social security. What is needed to establish my position?
Direct answer

Social-security coverage is compulsory by law. The worker may submit an inspection complaint to the Retirement and Social Security Department to require registration, retroactive contributions and statutory fines, with the service counted for employment and retirement purposes.

Legal reference

Articles 14 onwards of Workers' Retirement and Social Security Law No. 18 of 2023.

Required documents

Evidence of commencing and performing work at the establishment, work ID details and an inspection complaint to the competent department.

82I want to resign. What should I review before giving notice?
Direct answer

Give the employer written resignation notice 30 days before leaving, as required by law, and establish receipt to avoid a compensation claim for breaching the notice period and to ensure an amicable settlement of outstanding payments.

Legal reference

Article 43 of Iraqi Labour Law No. 37 of 2015.

Required documents

A formal written resignation, management's signed acknowledgement and a dated, stamped copy retained by the worker.

83My employment contract contains a penalty or non-compete clause. What determines its effect?
Direct answer

A non-compete clause is valid only where the worker has access to the establishment's secrets and the restriction is limited by time, place and type of work. The court may invalidate or reduce it where it infringes the constitutional right to work or is excessive or abusive.

Legal reference

Article 40 of Iraqi Labour Law No. 37 of 2015 and Article 216 of the Civil Code.

Required documents

The employment contract and a precise review of the restriction's wording, duration, geographical scope and type of activity.

84I am a foreign worker in Iraq with an employment dispute. Which documents does a lawyer need?
Direct answer

The passport, entry visa, Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs work permit and employment contract are needed to bring a Labour Court claim without fear of threats to terminate residence or impose arbitrary deportation contrary to law.

Legal reference

Articles 30 onwards of Labour Law No. 37 of 2015 and Foreigners Residency Law No. 76 of 2017.

Required documents

The valid work permit, official residence document, employment contract and a statement of unpaid wages and entitlements.

85There is a dispute about working hours or leave. What information can support a claim?
Direct answer

Ordinary working hours are eight per day and 48 per week. Excess hours are overtime payable at enhanced rates of 150% for daytime work and 200% at night and on holidays. Electronic attendance records or written instructions establish the work performed.

Legal reference

Articles 55–67 and 72–83 of Iraqi Labour Law No. 37 of 2015.

Required documents

Electronic attendance records, overtime instructions and a record of statutory leave denied.

156An employee sued my business. Which records should I prepare for my lawyer?
Direct answer

Prepare signed payroll records, electronic attendance records, warnings and investigation reports preceding dismissal, and proof of payment of all entitlements or a lawful termination reason under Article 42 of the Labour Law, to defend the claim.

Legal reference

Articles 42, 43 and 47 of Iraqi Labour Law No. 37 of 2015.

Required documents

The employment contract, wage receipts, attendance and absence records, and internal misconduct investigation minutes.

157I am being asked to sign a resignation or waiver against my wishes. What should be reviewed?
Direct answer

Do not sign. If signed under psychological coercion or a threat of dismissal, it can be challenged and coercion proved by all evidentiary means, including witnesses and messages. The resignation is invalid and the worker is reinstated with compensation.

Legal reference

Articles 112–116 of the Civil Code on defects of consent and coercion, and Labour Law No. 37 of 2015.

Required documents

Threat or pressure recordings and messages, testimony from colleagues present, and an immediate written explanation and withdrawal of resignation.

164An employer retains a foreign worker's passport and refuses to return it. What information determines the appropriate action?
Direct answer

Withholding a passport is unlawful and constitutes appropriation of an official document. The worker or lawyer may complain urgently to the Investigation Court judge or labour inspection section to compel immediate, unconditional return and impose a fine on the employer.

Legal reference

Iraqi Passports Law No. 32 of 2015 and Labour Law No. 37 of 2015.

Required documents

A copy of the withheld passport, the employment contract and a formal notice or complaint to the police and labour inspectorate.
