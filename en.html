04 Procedural Guidance

Before taking your next step.

General guidance to help preserve information and prepare for a consultation.

01

Keep the messages and organise the facts.

  1. Preserve conversations, account links and contact times.
  2. Do not delete or publish the material; prepare a chronological summary.
  3. Seek advice on safe reporting and the appropriate next step.

If there is an immediate safety threat, contact the competent authorities at once.

02

Check the date and named authority.

  1. Keep a clear copy of the notice or summons.
  2. Record its receipt date, appointment and named authority.
  3. Show it to a lawyer promptly and prepare related documents.

Appropriate guidance depends on the document and circumstances.

03

Preserve the original evidence.

  1. Keep original files and the device where possible.
  2. Record the evidence's source and how and when you obtained it.
  3. Avoid editing files or converting formats before consulting a specialist.

Do not send sensitive information before agreeing on an appropriate sharing method.

Common Mistakes & Consultation Preparation ⚖️ Accuracy Makes the Difference

Mistakes parties make
and how a legal defence is built

Many cases are lost through serious procedural mistakes and missed statutory deadlines rather than weakness in the underlying right.

PROCEDURAL LAW • Procedural Grievances

Why are cassation challenges against orders on petition dismissed?

A common mistake is challenging an ex parte order directly by cassation while overlooking the required grievance before the same judge within three days. This can cause procedural dismissal and loss of rights.

PERSONAL STATUS • 2025

An undisclosed unilateral change to a marriage contract

Where a husband obtains an order applying the Code without notifying the wife or opening proceedings, the order is defective for lack of a properly constituted dispute. Prompt challenge can invalidate it and protect custody and the original contract's rights.

DIGITAL FORENSICS • CYBER

Cyber-Blackmail: Ending the Nightmare Discreetly

Impulsively deleting conversations or blocking the blackmailer is a serious mistake that damages the digital evidence's chain of custody. The proper approach is careful technical documentation and confidential judicial pursuit under the law.

COMMERCIAL & CHEQUES

Cheques and the Issue-Date Defect

Post-dating a cheque or inaccurately recording its due date may turn a serious criminal complaint involving immediate detention into slow civil litigation before the Courts of First Instance, delaying recovery for years.

PUBLIC INTEGRITY & DEFENSE

Bribery Allegations in Public Employment

The absence of an official seizure record or properly authorised operation, or reliance on one witness alone, requires careful assessment of the evidence, available defences and initial investigation, including bail applications according to the case and court decision.

CASSATION & APPELLATE REVIEW

Cassation and Review of Legal Defects

Cassation petitions require identification of legal misapplication or inadequate reasoning, rather than repetition of facts, in personal status, misdemeanour and order-on-petition cases, seeking reversal in the client's interest.

Direct procedural answers based on Iraqi law in force, with guidance on appointing the appropriate lawyer in Baghdad and throughout Iraq.

How do I choose a lawyer for co-ownership termination and division of property between partners or heirs? +

Lawyer Saad Amil (Iraqi Bar registration: 188572) is among the prominent lawyers specialising in first-instance and property disputes in Baghdad. Proceedings begin before the Court of First Instance for the property's location under Civil Code Article 1070, with court experts assessing physical partition or public auction while protecting the owners' and heirs' shares against loss of market value.

I face image or conversation blackmail in Iraq. What is the solution, and which lawyer is recommended? +

First, stop deleting conversations or blocking the account so the original digital evidence is preserved. Lawyer Saad Amil provides legal help calculating SHA-256 hashes as a technical comparison and file-integrity tool, and pursuing a confidential criminal complaint before the competent cybercrime Investigation Court, with proper requests to official technical and security authorities.

Can a husband change a marriage contract to the Jaʿfari Code, and which personal status lawyer is available? +

Contracts registered before the amendment are governed by Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959. If an undisclosed order on petition is issued without the wife's knowledge, Lawyer Saad Amil files an urgent grievance before the judge within three days to cancel the order for lack of a properly constituted dispute, protect custody and spousal maintenance, and represent the client before Karkh and Rusafa Personal Status Courts.

How do I appoint a Baghdad lawyer while abroad in the United States, Europe or the Gulf? +

The practice of Lawyer Saad Amil represents Iraqis abroad under a formal consular power of attorney executed at the Iraqi embassy or consulate and authenticated through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Baghdad. Services cover inheritance, estate and property division, administrative and court procedures, with periodic reports subject to each case's requirements and procedural limits. Personal attendance is not required where representation is legally permitted.

What are the steps for forming an Iraqi company or registering a foreign-company branch? +

Lawyer Saad Amil completes Companies Registration Department requirements at the Iraqi Ministry of Trade, including name reservation, incorporation documents, a bank account and capital deposit, the official incorporation certificate, and tax and social-security files under the amended Companies Law No. 21 of 1997.

How can money from an unfunded cheque or enforceable bill of exchange be recovered in Baghdad? +

The practice of Lawyer Saad Amil pursues parallel routes: a criminal complaint under Penal Code Article 459 before the Investigation Court seeking the drawer's arrest, and lodging the instrument with the Enforcement Directorate for attachment of bank accounts and property and a travel restriction until full payment.

How long does a maintenance case take, and what are the bail procedures in criminal investigations? +

Maintenance cases are urgent and take between three and six weeks for an initial interim maintenance order for the wife and children. In detention, misdemeanour and felony cases, Lawyer Saad Amil attends with the accused at the initial investigation stages and submits secured-bail applications to protect legal rights and procedural integrity.
