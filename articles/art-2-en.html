Legal Protection for Iraqi Expatriates' Property in the United States and Europe

✍️ By: Lawyer Saad Amil (ID 188572) 📅 Published: 2026-11-01 📍 Baghdad, Republic of Iraq
Consular power-of-attorney authentication, electronic authenticity verification and protection of expatriates' land and property against estate-agent forgery.

Iraqis living in the United States, Canada, Britain and European countries face significant challenges managing property and estates in Baghdad and other governorates.

The first step is preparing a general or specific power of attorney at the Iraqi consulate in the country of residence, followed by electronic verification of its issuance through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Baghdad.

Our practice provides high-level court representation with personal supervision and documented photographic reports throughout the case, sparing expatriates the burden of travel. Consultation fees may be paid to an official US bank account by ACH or wire transfer.
