Digital Evidence and Chain of Custody in Cyber-Blackmail Cases

✍️ By: Lawyer Saad Amil (ID 188572) 📅 Published: 2026-10-29 📍 Baghdad, Republic of Iraq
Documenting SHA-256 forensic hashes for audio recordings and blackmail conversations for acceptance as conclusive evidence before the Investigation Court judge.

As deepfake and AI voice-cloning tools develop, criminal courts and Investigation Courts in Iraq increasingly require rigorous scientific examination of digital evidence.

Under Criminal Procedure Law No. 23 of 1971 and amended Iraqi Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979, electronic evidence is accepted only if its chain of custody has been preserved from extraction.

Drawing on our experience at Gold Stone Intelligence, our practice provides local forensic examination of recordings and extraction of cryptographic hashes to address allegations of fabrication by the opposing party.
