A Procedural Guide to Terminating Co-ownership and Dividing Property Shares in Iraqi Courts

✍️ By: Lawyer Saad Amil (ID 188572) 📅 Published: 2026-11-03 📍 Baghdad, Republic of Iraq
Steps for co-ownership proceedings before the competent Court of First Instance and protection of a partner's or heir's share against an undervalued auction sale.

A co-ownership termination claim under Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951 is one of the principal property claims used to resolve disputes between co-owners and heirs.

The claim is brought before the Court of First Instance with territorial jurisdiction over the property. A judicial expert assesses whether division in kind is possible. If it is not, the court orders a public auction and distribution of the sale proceeds according to each owner's inheritance or contractual share.

Lawyer Saad Amil recommends recording a restriction on disposal with the Property Registration Directorate as soon as the claim is filed, to prevent transfers of shares or manipulation of ownership.
