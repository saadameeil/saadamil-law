171An expert's property or damages valuation seems wrong. What is needed to assess an objection?
Direct answer

Submit a written, reasoned objection within the statutory period, three days to one week after notification or the hearing. Identify deficiencies, unfairness and departures from market prices, and request a panel of three or five experts for a new inspection.

Legal reference

Articles 138–140 of Iraqi Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979.

Required documents

The disputed court expert's report, comparable neighbouring property prices and a technical objection.

172The other party holds the original contract or document and refuses to produce it. How does this affect proof?
Direct answer

Ask the judge to compel production under Article 28 of the Evidence Law. If the opponent refuses or denies possession despite supporting indications, this may constitute judicial evidence favouring the claimant, or the document's contents may be proved through personal evidence, witnesses and a decisive oath.

Legal reference

Articles 28–35 of Iraqi Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979.

Required documents

A photocopy, email or notification, and an application identifying the opponent's interest in concealing the document.

173My case is repeatedly adjourned, and I do not know why. Which papers will a lawyer review?
Direct answer

The lawyer obtains copies of the entire case file and hearing minutes to examine whether adjournment concerns an expert report, record authentication, delayed service or a stay linked to another case under Article 83 of the Civil Procedure Law, and applies to expedite proceedings and determination.

Legal reference

Articles 59, 60 and 83 of the amended Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

The case number and court name, the lawyer's power of attorney and direct inspection of the file at the court registry.

174We settled during proceedings. What must be specified to document the settlement and its effect?
Direct answer

Submit the written settlement at a hearing or record the parties' acknowledgement of its terms in the minutes. The court approves the settlement and ends the dispute; the approving judgment is a final, enforceable instrument that is not subject to appeal.

Legal reference

Articles 144 and 145 of Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969 and Article 705 of the Civil Code.

Required documents

The settlement signed by the parties or representatives expressly authorised to settle, and identity documents.

175I want to change my lawyer. Which information, deadlines and documents must be arranged to maintain the case?
Direct answer

Formally remove the former lawyer through the court or notary public, or record the lawyer's withdrawal, and notify the Iraqi Bar Association under professional rules. Appoint the new lawyer, obtain the complete document file and hearing minutes, and confirm the next hearing to avoid cancellation of the proceedings.

Legal reference

The amended Iraqi Advocates Law No. 173 of 1965 and the Civil Procedure Law.

Required documents

The removal letter or withdrawal agreement, the new lawyer's power of attorney and receipt of the case papers from the court registry.
