124I was injured in a road accident. Which documents are needed to assess compensation?
Direct answer

The traffic police accident diagram, the final criminal conviction of the at-fault driver, the final forensic medical report stating physical disability, and treatment expenses are needed for a material and non-material damages claim before the Court of First Instance.

Legal reference

Articles 202–207 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951 and the Traffic Law.

Required documents

The accident diagram, final criminal conviction and final forensic medical disability report.

125I believe medical negligence harmed me. Which reports and facts does a lawyer need?
Direct answer

The complete medical file, hospital operation and medication records, and a specialist advisory medical committee report from the Ministry of Health and Iraqi Medical Association establishing departure from accepted scientific standards and its direct causal link to deterioration or disability.

Legal reference

Articles 202 and 204 of the Iraqi Civil Code, the Medical Association Law and the Medical Liability Law.

Required documents

Patient and hospital records, the forensic medical report and recommendations of official medical investigation committees.

126A contractor left defective construction or abandoned work. Which evidence is needed?
Direct answer

Immediately bring an urgent inspection and evidence-preservation action to document structural defects, actual completion and repair costs through a court expert. Then claim compensation and apply the ten-year building-safety warranty.

Legal reference

Articles 868–872 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The construction contract, payment receipts, urgent judicial inspection report and valuation of remedial and repair costs.

127A fire or leak from a neighbouring property damaged my home or shop. What determines responsibility?
Direct answer

Consider the building custodian's liability. Establish the source of the damage through a Civil Defence expert's fire or leak report, seek an urgent judicial inspection to assess losses, and claim full compensatory damages before the Court of First Instance.

Legal reference

Articles 219 and 231 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

Criminal-evidence and Civil Defence reports, the urgent evidence-preservation inspection and invoices for damaged items.

128The insurer refused my claim or disputes the amount. Which documents require review?
Direct answer

Review the insurance policy, coverage and exclusions, and proof of notification within the contractual period. Challenge an unfair refusal before the Court of First Instance and seek the full indemnity with interest.

Legal reference

Articles 982–1007 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The insurance policy, police reports and incident evidence, and the insurer's refusal letter and reasons.

129A product or service caused me financial loss. What determines liability and a possible claim?
Direct answer

Establish the latent defect or non-conformity, the purchase invoice and date, and make a formal complaint to the Consumer Protection Council. Bring contractual and tort claims for repayment and compensation for consequential damage.

Legal reference

Iraqi Consumer Protection Law No. 1 of 2010 and Articles 558 onwards of the Civil Code.

Required documents

The official purchase invoice, defective goods or technical examination report, and evidence of resulting losses and damage.

130Someone harmed my reputation or business. What is needed to prove harm and assess compensation?
Direct answer

Establish the wrongful or defamatory act and a final conviction for defamation, insult or unfair competition. Prove material harm such as reduced sales or cancelled contracts and non-material harm to standing and reputation, to seek fair compensation.

Legal reference

Articles 202 and 205 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The final criminal conviction, offending publications or recordings and evidence of resulting business losses.

176My car was damaged or parts went missing at a repair workshop. Which evidence establishes responsibility?
Direct answer

The craftsperson or professional is liable for wrongdoing and negligence. Immediately obtain a police report recording the vehicle's condition, damage and missing parts. Claim the damage, parts value and loss of use before the Court of First Instance, or complain of breach of trust if parts were deliberately substituted.

Legal reference

Articles 873 onwards of the Civil Code on contractors' and craftspeople's obligations, and Article 202 of the Civil Code.

Required documents

The workshop receipt, a police condition report and invoices valuing damage and stolen parts.

177A road accident killed a family member. Which documents identify the claims and responsible parties?
Direct answer

Obtain the accident diagram and the driver's conviction from the Traffic or Misdemeanour Court, then an inheritance certificate. Claim material and non-material damages for loss of a provider before the Court of First Instance against the driver and vehicle owner, and seek compulsory insurance payment from the National Insurance Company.

Legal reference

Compulsory Motor Accident Insurance Law No. 52 of 1980 and Articles 202–205 of the Civil Code.

Required documents

The accident diagram, death certificate, final criminal conviction, inheritance certificate and a guardianship certificate where minors are involved.

178A hospital or medical centre refuses a copy of my medical file. What is needed to obtain it?
Direct answer

The medical file is the patient's legal right. Serve a notarial notice on the hospital, request a First Instance or Investigation Court order compelling production, or refer the matter to Ministry of Health inspection and the Medical Association to secure the file judicially against alteration.

Legal reference

Public Health Law No. 89 of 1981 and Iraqi Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979.

Required documents

Proof of hospital attendance or admission, the notarial notice and an urgent court application.

179After buying a car I found a significant undisclosed defect. What determines a possible claim?
Direct answer

Under the latent-defect remedy in Article 558 of the Civil Code, an old, material, hidden defect not disclosed by the seller permits the buyer, within the statutory period, to seek rescission, return the car and recover the full price, or seek a proportionate reduction with compensation. Establish the defect through a technical vehicle inspection.

Legal reference

Articles 558–570 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The purchase contract, a technical report establishing that the defect predated purchase, and an immediate notarial notice to the seller upon discovery.

180A carrier damaged my shipment or delivered it short. Which contract and documents are needed to assess compensation?
Direct answer

The carrier guarantees the goods' safety from receipt to delivery unless force majeure or an inherent defect is established. Record any shortage immediately on receipt and prepare a report, then claim the damaged goods' value and price difference before the Court of First Instance.

Legal reference

Articles 24–45 of the amended Iraqi Transport Law No. 80 of 1983.

Required documents

The waybill, delivery receipt with reservations, a damage or shortage report and the original goods invoices.
