36I co-own property, but the other owners refuse division or sale. Which options are available?
Direct answer

Bring an action to terminate co-ownership before the competent Court of First Instance. The court arranges an engineering inspection. If physical division is possible, the shares are separated; otherwise the property is sold by judicial public auction and the proceeds are distributed according to each owner's share.

Legal reference

Articles 1070 onwards of the Civil Code and Article 137 of the Civil Procedure Law.

Required documents

A recent property registration document showing the co-owners and their shares, and the property's location for the cadastral inspection.

37I bought property under an unregistered private contract. What determines my legal position?
Direct answer

Private property contracts made outside the registration process are formally invalid before the Real Estate Registration Department. However, the buyer may seek a judicial transfer of title under amended Revolutionary Command Council Decision No. 1198 of 1977 where its conditions are met, including possession, residence, construction and payment, or recover the entire price with compensation.

Legal reference

Decision No. 1198 of 1977 of the dissolved Revolutionary Command Council and Article 508 of the Civil Code.

Required documents

The original private purchase contract, payment receipts, and evidence of possession and construction.

38The seller took payment but refuses to transfer title. Which documents does a lawyer need?
Direct answer

Bring a judicial title-transfer action under Decision No. 1198 of 1977, or a claim for repayment of the price, compensation, the market-price difference and lost benefit under the rules governing breach of a promise to sell, following a notarial demand.

Legal reference

Decision No. 1198 of 1977 and Article 168 of Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The preliminary sale contract, receipts signed by the seller and a notarial notice requiring the seller to attend for registration.

39I want to buy property. How should its legal status be checked before paying a deposit?
Direct answer

Obtain a recent Form 25 extract from the competent Real Estate Registration Department to establish the true owner and confirm the absence of attachments, mortgages and restrictive entries. Check conformity with the municipal master plan and ensure there is no encroachment or compulsory acquisition.

Legal reference

Real Estate Registration Law No. 43 of 1971 and Municipalities Administration Law No. 165 of 1964.

Required documents

The plot and cadastral district numbers, a recent title extract and verification through municipal and cadastral site inspections.

40A tenant does not pay rent or refuses to hand back the property. Which procedure needs to be considered?
Direct answer

Serve a formal notarial notice requiring payment within 15 days of service. If the period expires without payment, bring an eviction action for non-payment, claim the arrears and enforce the eviction compulsorily.

Legal reference

Article 17(1) of the amended Property Rent Law No. 87 of 1979.

Required documents

The original written lease, the notarial notice and proof of service, and the leased property's title document.

41My landlord wants to evict me, but I have a lease. What determines our respective rights?
Direct answer

The tenant benefits from statutory protection and continuation of the lease under the Property Rent Law. The landlord may seek eviction only on a specified statutory ground, such as non-payment, demolition for reconstruction or the owner's urgent housing need subject to its strict conditions.

Legal reference

Articles 3 and 17 of the amended Property Rent Law No. 87 of 1979.

Required documents

The current lease, receipts showing regular rent payments and evidence of compliance with the lease terms.

42I bought an apartment in a development, but delivery is delayed. What is needed to assess performance or compensation claims?
Direct answer

Review the contract with the investment company, establish the agreed completion date, apply the delay penalty clause, serve a notarial notice and bring a claim for completion and delivery with compensation and the rental-cost difference.

Legal reference

Articles 168, 170 and 255 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The investment and purchase contract, receipts for all instalments paid and a site report recording the project's completion percentage.

43I am about to sign a lease. Which terms should a lawyer review first?
Direct answer

The landlord's status, ownership and authority to lease; the rent and payment dates; residential or commercial use; maintenance obligations; responsibility for fees and services; and renewal, termination and compensation provisions.

Legal reference

Property Rent Law No. 87 of 1979 and the Iraqi Civil Code.

Required documents

The proposed lease, the landlord's title document, both parties' identity documents and the handover terms.

44I discovered that a property was sold to more than one buyer. What should be checked first?
Direct answer

Priority in real estate registration governs: ownership belongs to the first person to register in the permanent register under Article 1279 of the Civil Code. Buyers holding private unregistered contracts may bring a criminal fraud complaint against the seller under Article 456 of the Penal Code and seek full compensation and repayment.

Legal reference

Article 1279 of the Civil Code and Article 456 of Penal Code No. 111 of 1969.

Required documents

The sequence of real estate registration entries, private sale contracts and receipts, and a detailed criminal complaint.

45Someone has encroached across my land boundary. Which documents and evidence are needed?
Direct answer

Bring an action to prevent interference and remove the encroachment. Request an urgent inspection with an accredited surveyor and the Real Estate Registration Department's surveying officer to overlay aerial and cadastral maps, identify the boundaries and establish the encroachment on site.

Legal reference

Articles 1070 and 1150 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The plot and cadastral district title document, the official property survey map, and photographs and witness evidence of the encroachment.

46My neighbour built something or opened an outlet that harms my property. What determines whether I can seek its removal?
Direct answer

Seek a court order closing the opening and removing abnormal harm. The law prohibits windows or light wells directly overlooking neighbouring property at less than the statutory distance, two metres for an overlooking window and one metre for a light well, and prohibits excessive nuisance that damages the neighbouring property.

Legal reference

Articles 1051, 1058 and 1059 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The title document and an urgent judicial inspection request to record the condition and distances and have an engineering expert assess the harm.

47My property title is missing or contains inconsistent information. What is needed for replacement or correction?
Direct answer

Apply to the competent Real Estate Registration Department for a replacement and complete the required newspaper publication and inspection procedures. If there is a substantive inconsistency in the register, bring a court action to correct the property classification or owner's name.

Legal reference

Real Estate Registration Law No. 43 of 1971, as amended.

Required documents

The plot and cadastral district numbers, the owner's national ID card, and confirmation from the microfilm records and property file.

48I want to buy property from someone acting under a power of attorney. What should be checked?
Direct answer

Check that the authority expressly includes sale, receipt of the price, acknowledgement of receipt and transfer through the Real Estate Registration Department. Obtain confirmation from the notary public that the power of attorney is authentic, that the agent has not been removed and that the principal has not died before signing any transaction.

Legal reference

Articles 927–947 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The original power of attorney, an official authenticity confirmation from the notary public's office and a recent life certificate for the principal.

49There is a property dispute, and I fear the other party will dispose of the property. Which interim measures can be considered?
Direct answer

Submit an urgent application to the Court of First Instance judge for a restriction on disposition or precautionary attachment to be entered against the property in the real estate register, preventing sale, transfer or mortgage until the underlying dispute is finally determined.

Legal reference

Articles 151 and 152 of the amended Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

The original claim, documents supporting the apparent right and payment of the required statutory security.

50I paid for a car, but ownership transfer was not completed. What determines how I can claim my rights?
Direct answer

Bring an action before the Court of First Instance against the registered owner and seller to compel transfer of the vehicle, with an attachment and restriction on disposition recorded at the General Traffic Directorate. If legal obstacles prevent transfer, seek rescission, repayment and compensation.

Legal reference

Traffic Law No. 8 of 2019 and Articles 508 onwards of the Civil Code.

Required documents

The private vehicle sale contract, the payment receipt, the vehicle registration document and proof of actual possession.

136After buying property, I discovered a mortgage or restrictive entry. What determines my rights against the seller?
Direct answer

Registered attachments and mortgages bind everyone and follow the property. The buyer may require their removal or seek rescission, full repayment, compensation and the price difference. A criminal fraud complaint under Article 456 of the Penal Code may also be possible where the seller deliberately concealed the entry.

Legal reference

Articles 558 and 1285 of the Iraqi Civil Code and Real Estate Registration Law No. 43 of 1971.

Required documents

The private purchase contract and receipts, a recent title extract showing the mortgage and a notarial notice to the seller.

137Property is still registered to someone who died several generations ago. Which documents establish the ownership succession?
Direct answer

Obtain successive Sharia and civil inheritance certificates for every deceased person from the first generation to the current living heirs. Then initiate an inheritance transfer with the Real Estate Registration Department, settle estate tax and devolution fees, and record the current heirs' shares in an official title.

Legal reference

Real Estate Registration Law No. 43 of 1971, the Personal Status Law and the Estate Tax Law.

Required documents

Death certificates for every generation, 1957 civil-register extracts, the complete inheritance certificates and the old property title.

138I refurbished a rented shop, and the owner now wants it vacated. What determines my rights to those costs?
Direct answer

Where improvements were made with the landlord's written consent, their existing value or the increase in property value may be recovered. Without consent, the tenant may remove them and restore the premises unless the landlord elects to retain them and pay their value as removable improvements, assessed by court experts.

Legal reference

Articles 747 and 748 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The lease and landlord's written approval, repair invoices and receipts, and an urgent judicial inspection recording the improvements.

139The developer changed the apartment's area or specifications. What is needed to assess a claim?
Direct answer

If a substantial shortfall or specification change reduces the agreed value or benefit, the buyer may seek rescission, repayment of instalments with interest and compensation, or a proportionate price reduction. Establish this through urgent evidence-preservation inspection and comparison with the plans.

Legal reference

Articles 542–546 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The purchase contract and attached plans, instalment receipts and a judicial survey report establishing the shortfall.
