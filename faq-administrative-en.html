86An administrative decision harmed me. Which dates and documents should I show a lawyer urgently?
Direct answer

The service date is critical. An administrative grievance must be lodged with the issuing authority within 30 days. If rejected, or if 30 days pass without a response, proceedings must be brought before the Administrative Court within the following 30 days to avoid the decision becoming unchallengeable and the right of challenge expiring.

Legal reference

Article 7 of the amended State Council Law No. 65 of 1979.

Required documents

The challenged administrative decision, its formal service date, and the grievance and acknowledgement of receipt.

87I received a disciplinary penalty or dismissal from public employment. What determines the challenge route?
Direct answer

Lodge an administrative grievance with the minister or head of the authority not attached to a ministry within 30 days of notification. If rejected, challenge before the Public Employees Court within 30 days, seeking cancellation or reduction of the unlawful disciplinary penalty.

Legal reference

Article 15 of the amended State and Public Sector Employees Discipline Law No. 14 of 1991.

Required documents

The administrative penalty order, administrative investigation minutes, the grievance and its receipt date.

88I have a dispute over salary, grade or promotion. Which papers are needed?
Direct answer

The appointment order, previous promotion and advancement decisions, annual performance evaluations and recognised educational qualification are needed. A decision withholding advancement or entitlement is challenged before the Public Employees Court under the State Employees Salaries Law.

Legal reference

The amended State and Public Sector Employees Salaries Law No. 22 of 2008.

Required documents

The official service summary, the entitlement order and evidence that all promotion requirements have been met.

89I was transferred or seconded and believe the decision harmed me. What needs to be reviewed?
Direct answer

Check whether the decision is a disguised punishment, an excess or abuse of power, or malicious action unrelated to the public interest. Challenge it before the Public Employees Court to seek cancellation and return to the original workplace.

Legal reference

Article 7 of State Council Law No. 65 of 1979 and Civil Service Law No. 24 of 1960.

Required documents

The transfer or secondment order, grounds of objection and evidence of the resulting personal and employment harm.

90My retirement application is stalled or contains discrepancies. Which documents should I prepare?
Direct answer

Provide the ministerial retirement order, authentication of the service summary and confirmation that pension contributions were paid. Challenge unfair National Pension Authority decisions before the Pensioners' Cases Review Board to obtain statutory entitlements.

Legal reference

Articles 21 onwards of the amended Unified Pension Law No. 9 of 2014.

Required documents

The ministerial retirement order, service record, the Pension Authority's rejection decision and a civil status or national ID card.

91I applied to a government authority but received no response. What determines my legal options?
Direct answer

Administrative silence for 30 days after registering a formal application or grievance constitutes an implied refusal or negative administrative decision. Once that period expires, the applicant may challenge it before the Administrative Court within the next 30 days.

Legal reference

Article 7(Eleventh) of the amended State Council Law No. 65 of 1979.

Required documents

The official application bearing the incoming-registration stamp and date, and evidence that the period passed without a reply.

92I have a contractual dispute with a government authority. What determines the competent forum?
Direct answer

Determine whether the contract includes an arbitration clause or places jurisdiction with the Courts of First Instance. Complete prior grievance and amicable communication requirements under the Government Contracts Implementation Instructions before bringing a compensation claim.

Legal reference

Government Contracts Implementation Instructions No. 2 of 2014 and the Iraqi Civil Code.

Required documents

The government contract, general and special conditions, official correspondence and handover and completion records.

93Some service was not counted, or my employment information is incorrect. What is needed to pursue correction?
Direct answer

Provide administrative orders for earlier service, whether contract work, daily-paid work, military service or professional practice such as law. Apply formally to the ministry for recognition for employment and pension purposes, and challenge any refusal before the Public Employees Court.

Legal reference

Civil Service Law No. 24 of 1960 and Contract Service Recognition Law No. 22 of 2019.

Required documents

Earlier commencement and release orders, salary records and evidence of contribution payments.

140I received a municipal notice to remove a building or encroachment. What should a lawyer review?
Direct answer

Review the title, master-plan boundaries and building permit. If the building lies within the property and complies with the rules, challenge the municipal decision before the Administrative Court or urgent-matters judge to stop demolition immediately, or regularise the violation through classification-correction committees and payment of the fine.

Legal reference

Municipalities Administration Law No. 165 of 1964 and the amended State Council Law No. 65 of 1979.

Required documents

The municipal notice and service date, building permit and approved plans, and a Form 25 title extract.

158My department summoned me before an investigative committee. What is needed to assess my position and defence?
Direct answer

Identify the allegation in the administrative order and avoid improvised statements. Use official documents and correspondence and record every defence in the investigation minutes for any later challenge before the Public Employees Court.

Legal reference

Articles 10 and 11 of the amended State and Public Sector Employees Discipline Law No. 14 of 1991.

Required documents

The order forming the investigative committee, correspondence relating to the alleged violation and exculpatory documents.

159My government appointment or contract application was rejected, and I believe the decision is wrong. What determines a challenge?
Direct answer

Review published selection criteria and entitlement scores. Lodge an administrative grievance with the issuing authority or Federal Public Service Council within 30 days of the results. If rejected, challenge before the Public Employees Court for cancellation and correction of the results.

Legal reference

Federal Public Service Council Law No. 4 of 2009 and the amended State Council Law No. 65 of 1979.

Required documents

The official application, selection scores, published successful applicants and the administrative grievance.

160A pensioner died, and there is a dispute over pension benefits. Which documents establish eligibility?
Direct answer

Pension payments do not follow ordinary estate and inheritance distribution. The Unified Pension Law governs payment only to eligible beneficiaries, including the widow, unmarried daughters, and minor or medically incapacitated sons; adult sons capable of work are excluded.

Legal reference

Articles 26–33 of the amended Unified Pension Law No. 9 of 2014.

Required documents

The death certificate, 1957 family register extract, Pension Authority beneficiary certificate and any medical incapacity reports.
