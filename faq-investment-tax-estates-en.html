359A GCC or foreign company plans to enter Iraq. Should it register a foreign company branch or incorporate an Iraqi LLC?
Direct answer

The optimal structure depends on activity scope: registering a foreign branch under Regulation No. 2 of 2017 links directly to the parent company's track record for government tenders and major projects, whereas incorporating an Iraqi LLC under amended Companies Law No. 21 of 1997 creates a standalone Iraqi legal entity.

Legal reference

Amended Companies Law No. 21 of 1997 and Foreign Company Branches Regulation No. 2 of 2017.

Required documents

Parent company incorporation certificate, articles, latest audited financial statements and board resolution authenticated by an Iraqi consulate and MOFA.

360What statutory incentives and protections does amended Iraqi Investment Law No. 13 of 2006 grant licensed investors?
Direct answer

Licensed investment projects enjoy a 10-year tax and fee exemption from commercial operation start (extendable with Iraqi partnership), customs-duty exemptions on imported project machinery and materials, repatriation of capital and profits, and guarantees against nationalisation or confiscation.

Legal reference

Amended Iraqi Investment Law No. 13 of 2006 (Articles 10–17).

Required documents

Economic and technical feasibility study, bank solvency and financing plan, and formal licence application to the National or Provincial Investment Commission.

361An Investment Commission threatened to revoke our investment licence alleging low completion progress. How is revocation contested?
Direct answer

Counsel audits whether the Commission and sectoral authorities fulfilled their prior obligations—delivering unencumbered land, external utilities and single-window permits. Delay caused by administrative obstruction cannot lawfully ground licence revocation under Articles 27–28 of Investment Law No. 13 of 2006.

Legal reference

Amended Investment Law No. 13 of 2006 (Articles 27 and 28).

Required documents

Investment licence, land lease/allocation agreement, site handover and progress minutes, and official single-window correspondence.

362Why do the Registrar of Companies and Tax Authority require a Bar-certified corporate legal counsel contract?
Direct answer

Iraqi corporate and advocacy legislation requires companies to appoint a practising Iraqi Bar Association lawyer as statutory legal adviser to ensure corporate governance compliance. A Bar-certified annual retainer is required to process general assembly minutes and annual regulatory filings.

Legal reference

Amended Companies Law No. 21 of 1997 and amended Advocates Law No. 173 of 1965.

Required documents

Company certificate of incorporation, managing director appointment resolution, and Bar-certified annual legal advisory agreement.

363What is the statutory deadline to object to an Iraqi income tax assessment, and what happens if it expires?
Direct answer

Under amended Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982, a written objection must be lodged within 21 days from service of the assessment notice. Missing this window without proven force majeure renders the assessment final; timely objection backed by CPA-audited accounts is critical.

Legal reference

Amended Iraqi Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (objection and appeal provisions).

Required documents

Tax assessment notice showing service date, CPA-certified financial statements, and vouchers proving deductible expenses.

364How are real-estate transfer tax and property rental tax assessed on high-value commercial buildings in Iraq?
Direct answer

Real-estate capital gains on title transfer are assessed under Decision No. 120 of 2002 (subject to statutory residential exemptions and base-value deductions), while annual commercial rental revenue is governed by amended Property Tax Law No. 162 of 1959. Inflated property valuations may be formally appealed.

Legal reference

Amended Property Tax Law No. 162 of 1959 and Decision No. 120 of 2002 on real-estate transfer tax.

Required documents

Tapu title deed, valuation inspection sheet, and registered commercial lease agreements.

365When does the Customs Court hear detained-cargo and customs cases, and is statutory settlement available?
Direct answer

Customs Courts established under amended Customs Law No. 23 of 1984 adjudicate customs offences and appeals against Director-General penalty decisions. The statute also permits a formal customs amicable settlement (Taswiya Sulhiya) with the Customs Authority to resolve the case and release goods.

Legal reference

Amended Iraqi Customs Law No. 23 of 1984.

Required documents

Customs seizure report, customs declaration and packing lists, and settlement application or Customs Court appeal petition.

366What is a Musataha contract under Iraqi Civil Code Article 1266, and why is Tapu registration mandatory?
Direct answer

Under Articles 1266–1270 of the Iraqi Civil Code, Musataha grants an in-rem right to construct commercial or industrial buildings on another's land for up to 50 years, with the holder owning the constructed buildings during the term. Registration in the Property Registration Directorate (Tapu) is mandatory to create the enforceable in-rem right.

Legal reference

Articles 1266–1270 of Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951 and Real Estate Registration Law No. 43 of 1971.

Required documents

Land title deed, comprehensive Musataha agreement specifying rent, term and post-expiry building ownership, and municipal permits.

367When a Musataha contract expires, who owns the commercial centre or building constructed on the land?
Direct answer

Under Civil Code Article 1269, ownership of constructed buildings upon expiry follows the parties' express terms in the registered Musataha contract (whether reverting to the landowner without payment or against agreed compensation); absent express stipulation, statutory valuation rules govern.

Legal reference

Articles 1268 and 1269 of amended Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

Tapu-registered Musataha agreement, updated property register extract, and engineering valuation reports.

368I contracted with a real-estate developer for commercial or residential investment units, and the developer halted work or altered plans. What are my remedies?
Direct answer

Counsel audits the investment unit sale contract and project licence; the buyer may sue for specific performance or rescission with full refund of instalments plus damages and lost profit under the Civil Code, alongside regulatory complaints before the Investment Commission.

Legal reference

Amended Investment Law No. 13 of 2006 and contractual-liability rules of Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

Executed investment unit purchase contract, all official payment receipts, and approved project architectural plans.

369I am an expatriate business owner with a commercial building in Baghdad and want to prevent power-of-attorney forgery. How do I protect title?
Direct answer

Arrange periodic physical audits of the Property Registration ledger by trusted counsel, never grant unrestricted general sales powers of attorney, and maintain notarised commercial leases. Upon any suspicious filing, counsel immediately seeks a judicial non-disposal restriction, title-cancellation suit and criminal forgery prosecution.

Legal reference

Real Estate Registration Law No. 43 of 1971, Civil Procedure Law, and Penal Code Articles 289–298.

Required documents

Original Tapu deed copy, National ID or passport, and a restricted protective power of attorney excluding any power of sale.

370How is a commercial agency for a foreign brand registered and legally protected in Iraq?
Direct answer

Commercial Agency Regulation Law No. 79 of 2017 requires the commercial agent to be an Iraqi national or Iraqi-owned company, and mandates authenticating the foreign principal's agency agreement through the Iraqi consulate and MOFA and registering it with the Registrar of Companies.

Legal reference

Iraqi Commercial Agency Regulation Law No. 79 of 2017.

Required documents

Consular- and MOFA-authenticated commercial agency contract, Iraqi company registration papers, and Chamber of Commerce membership.

371Counterfeit goods bearing our registered trademark entered the Iraqi market. How do we seize them and claim damages?
Direct answer

Under amended Trademarks and Commercial Descriptions Law No. 21 of 1957, the registered owner may obtain precautionary seizure of counterfeit goods in markets or at customs borders, pursue criminal trademark-counterfeiting charges, and sue for injunctive relief and damages before the Court of First Instance.

Legal reference

Amended Trademarks Law No. 21 of 1957 and Customs Law No. 23 of 1984.

Required documents

Valid Iraqi trademark registration certificate, samples and purchase invoices of counterfeit goods, and power of attorney.

372My partner in an Iraqi LLC monopolises management, withholds profits and blocks access to accounts. What legal actions exist?
Direct answer

Under amended Companies Law No. 21 of 1997, a shareholder may petition the Registrar of Companies for formal inspection of company books, challenge unlawful general assembly resolutions, sue for partner accounting and profit distribution, and seek removal of the managing director for serious breaches.

Legal reference

Amended Iraqi Companies Law No. 21 of 1997.

Required documents

Company articles of association and share certificate, available annual balance sheets, and formal notices served on the managing director.
