314When does the Second General Amnesty Amendment Law No. 2 of 2025 cover narcotics possession, and when is coverage excluded?
Direct answer

The law covers possession of narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances strictly for personal use where the net forensic laboratory weight does not exceed 50 grams, provided the person was not previously granted amnesty or previously convicted of a drug offence. International and domestic trafficking, distribution and cultivation under Articles 27 and 28 remain excluded.

Legal reference

Second General Amnesty Amendment Law No. 2 of 2025 (Official Gazette issue 4814 of 18/02/2025) and Narcotics Law No. 50 of 2017.

Required documents

Certified judgment copy, forensic laboratory report specifying net weight and substance type, and criminal record extract.

315How is an application for re-investigation or retrial filed under the 2025 Amnesty Law for a coerced confession or secret informant?
Direct answer

Defence counsel files a reasoned petition before the judicial committee at the relevant Court of Appeal requesting re-investigation or retrial where conviction rested on an allegedly coerced confession, a confidential informant or an uncorroborated co-defendant statement. The committee reviews the original file, medical reports and evidentiary inconsistencies.

Legal reference

Law No. 2 of 2025, Article 37(1)(c) of the Iraqi Constitution, and Articles 125 and 127 of Criminal Procedure Law No. 23 of 1971.

Required documents

Power of attorney, certified conviction, sentence and cassation decisions, medical examination reports recording coercion injuries if available, and statement transcripts.

316The General Amnesty Committee rejected an application for coverage or re-investigation. Can that decision be challenged?
Direct answer

A refusal by an amnesty judicial committee is not final on its own; it may be challenged by cassation before the Federal Court of Cassation within the 30-day statutory period from formal notice, through a reasoned petition demonstrating misapplication of Law No. 2 of 2025.

Legal reference

General Amnesty Law No. 27 of 2016 as amended by Law No. 2 of 2025, and Executive Instructions No. 1 of 2025.

Required documents

Copy of the committee's refusal decision showing service date, underlying judgment, and a reasoned cassation petition.

317Can public-funds offences qualify for General Amnesty if the financial loss is repaid?
Direct answer

Where a public-funds offence is eligible under the statute, amnesty coverage strictly requires full restitution or approved legal settlement of all assessed public-funds losses with the harmed state entity and official financial clearance prior to release.

Legal reference

General Amnesty Law No. 27 of 2016 as amended by Law No. 2 of 2025.

Required documents

Judgment specifying the financial loss, official assessment from the harmed state entity, and official payment receipts and clearance.

318Which evidentiary criteria distinguish drug trafficking (Article 28) from possession for personal use (Article 32)?
Direct answer

Classification does not rest on weight alone. Courts examine indicia of commercial intent: precision scales, retail packaging, cash proceeds, phone messages evidencing sales, biological blood/urine test results confirming personal use, and search warrant legality. Where commercial indicators are absent, defence counsel seeks reclassification to Article 32.

Legal reference

Articles 27, 28 and 32 of Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Law No. 50 of 2017.

Required documents

Seizure and search record, forensic phone examination report, biological toxicology report and chemical laboratory weight analysis.

319Security forces searched a home or vehicle without a judicial warrant and outside flagrante delicto. How does this affect the case?
Direct answer

Article 17 of the Iraqi Constitution and Articles 72–86 of the Criminal Procedure Law prohibit residential searches without a prior judicial warrant outside strict flagrante delicto situations. An unlawful search invalidates evidence derived solely from that breach.

Legal reference

Article 17 of the Iraqi Constitution and Articles 43 and 72–86 of Criminal Procedure Law No. 23 of 1971.

Required documents

Arrest and search record showing exact timestamps, prior or subsequent judicial orders, and seizure-witness statements.

320I leased my car under a formal contract, the lessee used it to transport drugs, and the court confiscated it. How do I recover my car?
Direct answer

File a third-party restitution claim and an independent cassation challenge against the confiscation clause under Penal Code Article 101, which protects all rights of bona-fide third parties. Establishing title and a genuine lease predating arrest without knowledge of the crime leads to reversal of confiscation.

Legal reference

Article 101 of amended Penal Code No. 111 of 1969 and Article 309 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

Required documents

Original vehicle registration card in the owner's name, formal lease agreement predating the arrest, and copy of the confiscation judgment.

321I operate a medical warehouse or chemical import firm facing scrutiny over controlled substances or precursors. What governs defence?
Direct answer

Import, storage and dispensing of medical psychotropics and chemical precursors are governed by Narcotics Law No. 50 of 2017, its schedules, and Instructions No. 2 of 2026. Defence relies on reconciling Ministry of Health import permits, batch ledgers and licensed pharmacy dispensing records.

Legal reference

Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Law No. 50 of 2017 and Precursor Instructions No. 2 of 2026.

Required documents

Valid warehouse or pharmacy licence, Ministry of Health import approvals, inventory ledgers and official medical prescriptions.

322When can defence counsel sever terrorist intent and transfer an Article 4 terrorism case to the ordinary Penal Code?
Direct answer

Where case facts show no motive to spread public terror, threaten national unity or attack state institutions, and instead demonstrate a personal, financial or localised dispute between specific parties, the Federal Court of Cassation transfers the offence to ordinary Penal Code provisions.

Legal reference

Articles 1–4 of Anti-Terrorism Law No. 13 of 2005 and Penal Code No. 111 of 1969.

Required documents

Initial and judicial investigation records, complainant and witness statements showing the personal or financial dispute, and site inspection report.

323What evidentiary weight does a secret informant or co-defendant statement carry without physical corroboration?
Direct answer

Under Articles 125 and 213 of the Criminal Procedure Law and settled Federal Court of Cassation rulings, statements by a confidential informant or co-defendant standing alone cannot sustain a serious felony conviction unless corroborated by independent material, forensic or eyewitness evidence.

Legal reference

Articles 125 and 213 of Criminal Procedure Law No. 23 of 1971.

Required documents

Secret informant statement record, separated co-defendant transcripts, and physical seizure or forensic examination reports.

324How does defence counsel establish legitimate self-defence of person or property in homicide or assault trials?
Direct answer

By proving the statutory elements under Penal Code Articles 42–46: an imminent unlawful attack against life or property, impossibility of immediate recourse to public authorities, and proportionality. Where limits were exceeded in good faith, Article 45 permits substantial sentence mitigation.

Legal reference

Articles 42–46 of amended Iraqi Penal Code No. 111 of 1969.

Required documents

Medical reports documenting injuries sustained by the accused, CCTV footage, crime-scene sketch and eyewitness accounts of who initiated the attack.

325When is an intentional homicide charge downgraded from Article 406 to Article 405 or fatal assault under Article 410?
Direct answer

It is downgraded to Article 405 when premeditation and Article 406 statutory aggravators are absent, and to Article 410 when weapon characteristics, wound location and autopsy pathology prove the accused intended bodily assault rather than death.

Legal reference

Articles 33, 405, 406 and 410 of amended Iraqi Penal Code No. 111 of 1969.

Required documents

Detailed forensic autopsy report, weapon seizure record and witness statements describing how the confrontation arose.

326A prisoner has three separate prison sentences. When does sentence merger apply versus the 25-year statutory cap?
Direct answer

Sentence merger under Penal Code Article 142 applies when offences committed for one criminal purpose are indivisibly linked, executing only the heaviest sentence. Where offences are independent, Article 143(A) caps total consecutive imprisonment executed at 25 years and absorbs detention within prison terms.

Legal reference

Articles 142 and 143(A) and (C) of amended Iraqi Penal Code No. 111 of 1969.

Required documents

Certified copies of all final judgments, correctional sentence-calculation sheet and a formal application to the competent Criminal Court.

327What are the exclusive statutory grounds for criminal retrial after cassation affirmation?
Direct answer

Article 270 of the Criminal Procedure Law restricts retrial to specific grounds, chiefly: discovery of decisive previously unknown facts or documents proving innocence, a final conviction establishing perjury or forgery of evidence underlying the verdict, or two irreconcilable final judgments on the same act. Applications pass through the Public Prosecution to the Court of Cassation.

Legal reference

Articles 270–279 of Criminal Procedure Law No. 23 of 1971.

Required documents

Final judgment and cassation affirmation, plus the newly discovered decisive document or final perjury/forgery judgment.

328When is a prisoner eligible for conditional release, and which convictions are excluded?
Direct answer

Under Article 331 of the Criminal Procedure Law, conditional release may be granted after serving three-quarters of the sentence (two-thirds for juveniles), minimum six months, upon verified good conduct in prison and payment of financial obligations, provided the offence is not among the statutory exclusions in Article 331(D).

Legal reference

Articles 331–337 of Criminal Procedure Law No. 23 of 1971, as amended.

Required documents

Conditional release petition, correctional administration good-conduct and term-calculation report, and receipts for paid fines or restitution.
