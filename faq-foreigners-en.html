210I am a foreign national who does not speak Arabic and have received an Iraqi court notice. What do I need to understand it and arrange translation and legal assistance?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Rule: Arabic is the mandatory judicial language of all Iraqi courts pursuant to Article (21) of the Civil Procedure Code No. 83 of 1969. Immediate Roadmap: Immediately transmit a clear image of the court notice (ورقة التبليغ) to counsel. The judicial calendar begins running upon service. Counsel arranges certified translation via a sworn legal translator, enters appearance on the scheduled hearing date, petitions the court for an adjournment to submit defense pleadings, and ensures an official court translator is appointed for any personal hearings under Article (61) of the Criminal Procedure Code.

211My foreign relative is detained in Iraq and our family is abroad. What information is needed for a lawyer to follow up and assess arrangements for consular contact?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Basis: Article (36) of the Vienna Convention on Consular Relations 1963 and the Iraqi Criminal Procedure Code No. 23 of 1971. Required Information: Full legal name, nationality, passport number, date and location of arrest, alleged criminal charge, and the designated detention facility or investigative court. Counsel enters an immediate appearance before the investigating judge, petitions for bail if legally permissible (Art. 109 CPC), inspects the investigative dossier, and notifies the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the foreign embassy to arrange official consular access.

212I was asked to give a statement or sign a record in Arabic, which I do not fully understand. What should a lawyer review before I sign?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Critical Judicial Warning: Never sign or place a fingerprint (بصمة إبهام) on any Arabic document you do not fully comprehend. A signature constitutes absolute legal presumption of knowledge and consent. Procedural Rights: Demand your constitutional right to: (a) The presence of your legal counsel; and (b) An official sworn interpreter to translate the deposition line by line. Counsel reviews the record to verify procedural compliance, eliminates coercive or misleading phrasing, and enters formal objections before any execution.

213My contract has Arabic and English versions that differ on the amount payable or the parties’ obligations. What should be checked before signing or making a claim?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Governing Rule: Under established Iraqi jurisprudence and state contracting guidelines, the Arabic text is the prevailing and legally controlling version before Iraqi courts in the event of conflict, unless the contract contains an explicit, unequivocal clause establishing the English text as the sole governing language. Counsel must audit the "Governing Language" and "Dispute Resolution" clauses to resolve textual discrepancies via a formal addendum prior to filing suit.

214I am a foreign tenant in Baghdad and my landlord refuses to return my security deposit. Which contract documents and evidence should I prepare to assess a claim?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Governing Statute: Lease contract covenants and the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951 (Arts. 742 et seq.). Required Evidence: Original lease agreement containing the explicit security deposit covenant; signed payment receipts or bank remittance slips; mutual handover inspection record (محضر تسليم المفاتيح) proving the property was vacated in good condition; and paid utility clearance slips (electricity/water). Counsel serves a formal Notarial Notice via the Notary Public (كاتب عدل) demanding return within a statutory deadline, followed by filing an action for recovery before the Court of First Instance (محكمة البداءة).

215I am a foreign national considering buying property or a share in a project in Iraq. What should be checked about my eligibility and the project before I make any payment?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Trap: Foreign property ownership is subject to severe statutory restrictions under Foreigners' Immovable Property Ownership Law No. 38 of 1961 (requiring strict reciprocity and Council of Ministers' approval), except for residential units within licensed investment projects under Investment Law No. 13 of 2006. Due Diligence Checklist: Verify title deed status (طابو صرف vs. agricultural/encumbered); ensure the contract is not an unnotarized "external contract" (عقد خارجي), which is void by law under Article (508) of the Civil Code; and inspect the developer's license with the National Investment Commission.

216I am a foreign national with a case or claim in Iraq and need to leave the country. What should be reviewed to determine whether the matter can continue through a power of attorney?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Procedural Rule: Litigation proceeds normally in your absence provided you execute a valid Judicial Power of Attorney (وكالة بالدعاوى) before an Iraqi Notary Public prior to departure. The instrument must expressly authorize counsel to represent, litigate, acknowledge, settle, appeal, collect funds, and enforce judgments through the Execution Directorate (مديرية التنفيذ).

217I am not Iraqi and live outside Iraq. What documents and power-of-attorney wording should be reviewed to appoint a lawyer for an Iraqi case?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Authentication Chain: Execute a Special Judicial Power of Attorney at the nearest Iraqi Embassy/Consulate using the digital Barcode system. If executed before a foreign notary: the instrument must be apostilled/legalized by the host state's Foreign Ministry, attested by the Iraqi Embassy abroad, couriered to Baghdad, and authenticated by the Iraqi Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Ministry of Justice. Ensure specific powers (settlement, collection, oath-taking, and registration) are explicitly detailed in Arabic.

218I represent a foreign company in a payment dispute with an Iraqi agent or distributor. What should be reviewed regarding the contract, correspondence and authority to represent the company?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Commercial Audit: Commercial Agency Registry: Verify if the agency is registered with the Commercial Agencies Department at the Ministry of Trade under Law No. 79 of 2017. Dispute Resolution Clause: Determine whether exclusive jurisdiction lies with Iraqi courts or an international arbitral tribunal (ICC, LCIA). Evidence Dossier: Certified invoices, Bills of Lading, signed balance confirmations (مطابقات أرصدة), and corporate resolutions empowering the company's legal representative.

219I had an accident while visiting Iraq and have a scheduled departure. What documents and procedures should be reviewed before I travel?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Emergency Roadmap: Ensure an official Police/Traffic Accident Report (مخطط الحادث ورسم الكروكي) is issued and filed with the competent investigative court. Undergo official forensic medical examination at a public hospital to secure a certified Medical-Legal Report (تقرير الطب العدلي). Grant a notarized Power of Attorney to your Iraqi legal counsel before traveling, enabling counsel to represent you as the civil/tort claimant for bodily injury and material compensation before the courts.

220I am a foreign national involved in a marriage or child-custody dispute connected to Iraq, and the other party has a different nationality. What facts and documents determine where the matter should be pursued?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Conflict of Laws: Articles (17–22) of the Iraqi Civil Code dictate jurisdiction based on the husband's nationality at the time of marriage, the place of marriage, and common habitual residence. If the defendant or the minor child resides within federal Iraq, Iraqi Personal Status Courts assert territorial jurisdiction to protect the best interests of the child (مصلحة المحضون الفضلى).

221My contract with an Iraqi party requires payment in a foreign currency and names a foreign court or arbitration forum. What should be reviewed before pursuing payment?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Constraints: Currency Regulations: Central Bank of Iraq (CBI) regulations require domestic performance to be settled in Iraqi Dinars (IQD) at official exchange rates. Arbitration Validity: Valid foreign arbitration clauses divest Iraqi courts of jurisdiction under Article (253) of the Civil Procedure Code if raised in the first hearing. Ensure foreign arbitral awards comply with the 1958 New York Convention or Law No. 30 of 1928 for local enforcement against Iraqi assets.
