Key legal safeguards for corporate funds, banks and contractors

Three operational rules preventing severe financial loss and missed statutory deadlines:

1. Protecting bank guarantees & halting withdrawal of work

Government construction and supply disputes are governed by Government Contracts Implementation Instructions No. 2 of 2014. Prompt formal documentation of site handover delays and unpaid progress certificates blocks guarantee confiscation, delay penalties and blacklisting.

2. Unfreezing bank accounts & proving lawful source of funds

In Central Bank of Iraq and Anti-Money Laundering Office holds under Law No. 39 of 2015, lifting freezes requires a structured evidentiary file linking transfers directly to supply contracts, customs declarations and audited financial statements.

3. Strict statutory deadlines in indemnification, tax & customs appeals

Administrative liability orders under Indemnification Law No. 31 of 2015 and arbitrary tax assessments carry strict, short statutory grievance and appeal windows before the Administrative Court and Tax Appeal Committees.

Procedural Questions, Statutory Defences & Practical Answers

Procedural answers supported by Iraqi legislation in force and Official Gazette citations:

My corporate or personal bank account in Iraq was frozen by a court or compliance order. How is the freeze lifted?

First we verify whether the freeze originated from the Economic Crime Investigation Court, the Anti-Money Laundering Office under Law No. 39 of 2015, or Central Bank compliance. Counsel submits a formal grievance and unfreezing petition backed by commercial contracts, invoices, customs declarations and tax clearance proving lawful source of funds; investigation court refusals may be challenged by cassation.

Source: Anti-Money Laundering Law No. 39 of 2015 and the Criminal Procedure Law
An Iraqi ministry threatened to withdraw work and call our bank guarantee despite delaying progress payments. What is the legal remedy?

Under Government Contracts Implementation Instructions No. 2 of 2014 and the Iraqi Civil Code, a contracting authority cannot lawfully impose delay fines, withdraw work or confiscate a performance bond where delay stems from administrative defaults such as obstructed site handover, delayed variation orders or unpaid progress certificates. We serve formal notice and seek an urgent court order staying guarantee liquidation alongside substantive contract litigation.

Source: Government Contracts Instructions No. 2 of 2014 and Civil Procedure Law Article 151
How do I challenge an administrative financial indemnification order issued under Indemnification Law No. 31 of 2015?

Indemnification Law No. 31 of 2015 and Instructions No. 2 of 2017 require filing a mandatory administrative grievance with the issuing minister or head of entity within the statutory period, followed by an administrative annulment lawsuit before the Administrative Court at the State Council challenging personal fault, causation and quantum assessment.

Source: Indemnification Law No. 31 of 2015 and amended State Council Law No. 65 of 1979
What is the difference between Penal Code Articles 340 and 341 in Federal Integrity Commission cases?

Penal Code Article 340 is a serious felony requiring proof that a public official intentionally caused damage to public funds or interests to secure an unlawful benefit, whereas Article 341 is a misdemeanour addressing grave damage caused by gross unintentional negligence. Disproving intentional criminal mens rea and proving adherence to administrative approvals defeats felony charges.

Source: Articles 340 and 341 of amended Iraqi Penal Code No. 111 of 1969
Are companies registered in Iraq required to appoint an official legal counsel?

Yes. Amended Companies Law No. 21 of 1997 and amended Advocates Law No. 173 of 1965 require companies operating in Iraq to appoint a practising lawyer registered with the Iraqi Bar Association under a Bar-certified annual legal advisory contract, which is mandatory for annual corporate filings and tax clearances.

Source: Amended Companies Law No. 21 of 1997 and Advocates Law No. 173 of 1965
The General Commission for Taxes issued an excessive arbitrary tax assessment against our company. How do we appeal?

Under amended Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982, the taxpayer files a written objection with the issuing tax authority within 21 days of service, supported by CPA-certified financial statements and source vouchers. Rejections may be appealed before the Tax Appeal Committee and escalated to the Tax Cassation Panel.

Source: Amended Iraqi Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982
What are the validity requirements for a Musataha contract to build a commercial centre in Iraq?

Article 1266 of Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951 defines Musataha as an in-rem right entitling the holder to construct buildings or installations (excluding plantings) on another's land for a term not exceeding 50 years. To establish a valid in-rem right enforceable against third parties, the contract must be registered with the Property Registration Directorate (Tapu).

Source: Article 1266 of amended Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951
How can I immediately freeze a defaulting debtor's bank accounts and properties before assets are transferred?

Articles 231–240 of Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969 empower a creditor holding an official or written debt instrument to obtain an urgent ex parte precautionary attachment over the debtor's properties, vehicles, bank balances and third-party receivables upon providing statutory security, provided the substantive debt and attachment-validation claim is filed within 8 days.

Source: Articles 231–240 of Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969
How does a foreign or GCC investor establish an official branch or licensed investment project in Baghdad?

Foreign investors may either register a foreign company branch with the Ministry of Trade's Registrar of Companies under Foreign Company Branches Regulation No. 2 of 2017, or obtain an investment licence under amended Investment Law No. 13 of 2006 granting tax/customs exemptions and land allocation, following consular and Iraqi Ministry of Foreign Affairs authentication of parent-company corporate documents.

Source: Amended Investment Law No. 13 of 2006 and Foreign Branches Regulation No. 2 of 2017
Our company's imported shipment was detained at an Iraqi customs port over a declaration dispute. What is the legal step?

We audit the customs seizure record under amended Customs Law No. 23 of 1984, apply for provisional cargo release against financial security to prevent spoilage and container demurrage charges, and represent the importer before the competent Customs Court or in statutory customs settlement proceedings.

Source: Amended Iraqi Customs Law No. 23 of 1984
