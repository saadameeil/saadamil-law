Cassation & Criminal Judgment Review

282My brother or son received a death sentence. What does a lawyer review first in the judgment and file?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Court composition and procedure: Three judges and a public prosecutor must be present, and appointed or retained counsel must attend every hearing under Article 144 of the Criminal Procedure Law. Conviction evidence: Compare the confession with the site inspection and reconstruction, and determine whether it stands alone or is corroborated. Medical and post-mortem reports: Compare the cause and time of death and weapon with the confession and witness statements. Coercion and torture: Check whether the accused complained of torture and whether medical referral was made or the request ignored.

283Is a death sentence automatically reviewed by cassation, or must we file a challenge? What is the lawyer's role?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Automatic review: Yes. Under Article 254(A) of the Criminal Procedure Law, the court must send the file to the Federal Court of Cassation even if nobody challenges it. Lawyer's role: Do not rely solely on automatic review, which may proceed without detailed rebuttal. The lawyer prepares a comprehensive cassation petition identifying invalidity, witness contradictions, inadequate investigation and incorrect legal classification for the General Panel. This can make the difference in quashing the judgment.

284What is the statutory cassation period for a death sentence, and when does it start in our case?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Period: Thirty days under Article 252(A) of the Criminal Procedure Law. Starting date exclusively under the criminal provision: For a judgment pronounced in the defendant's presence, the period starts the following day, as expressly provided in Article 252(A). For an in-absentia judgment, it starts when the judgment is deemed equivalent to one issued in the defendant's presence after the service periods expire.

285How does review of a death sentence differ between a judgment in the defendant's presence and one in absentia?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Judgment in the defendant's presence: An existing judgment final at the local level, reviewable only through the Federal Court of Cassation for cancellation or quashing. In-absentia judgment: Under Article 247 of the Criminal Procedure Law, the entire judgment falls and the earlier trial is treated as never having occurred when the convicted person is arrested or surrenders. A fresh trial in the person's presence starts before the same court.

286A previous lawyer challenged the death sentence. Can another lawyer review the file and submit additional grounds?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Yes. The accused or family may remove the representative or appoint another lawyer at any stage. Additional grounds: The new lawyer may submit supplementary grounds to the Federal Court of Cassation while the case remains undecided by a final decision.

287The death sentence relied on a confession said to have been coerced. How is this reviewed after judgment?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Authority: Article 127 of the Criminal Procedure Law and Article 37(First)(C) of the Constitution invalidate a confession obtained by force. Review: Examine the time between arrest and the statement before the Investigation Court judge, search the records for torture complaints and compare the initial detention medical report. Seek exclusion under Article 218 of the Criminal Procedure Law because the confession was vitiated by coercion.

288A death sentence was issued despite contradictory witnesses. When is contradiction a material ground?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Contradiction justifies quashing where it concerns the incident's substance or essential offence elements, such as the killer's identity, weapon, time, or incompatibility with the scene and technical report. It is also material where testimony is hearsay or based on an earlier personal dispute.

289The death sentence relied on a confidential informant or another accused person. What must a lawyer check?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Confidential informant: Information for investigative guidance alone is insufficient for a death conviction without compelling material evidence. One accused against another under Article 125 of the Criminal Procedure Law: The Court of Cassation treats such testimony as compromised because it may deflect or reduce blame. It cannot alone constitute conclusive evidence for death without other certain corroboration.

290We believe a forensic medical, laboratory or imaging report contradicts the death sentence. How is the conflict presented?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Present it in the cassation petition as a substantive ground: inadequate investigation and a decisive conflict between technical and oral evidence. Explain that the trial court ignored findings such as firing distance, projectile angle or actual cause of death, and seek quashing and referral to a five-member appellate forensic medical committee to establish the truth.

291The court imposed death without addressing an important document or defence in the file. How is the omission assessed?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Classify the omission as an express denial of defence rights and inadequate reasoning. Criminal procedure requires the court to address every substantive defence, including an alibi or a request to hear key prosecution or defence witnesses. Failure renders the judgment invalid and liable to mandatory quashing.

292The victim's family withdrew their claim after the death sentence. Can this affect the sentence?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal effect: Withdrawal does not extinguish the public prosecution in intentional homicide, but is a judicial mitigating circumstance under Articles 130 and 132 of the Penal Code. Application: Submit the withdrawal instrument and tribal settlement to the Court of Cassation if review is pending, or seek reconsideration to replace death with life imprisonment or a fixed term of 15 years.

293We challenged a death sentence. Does this automatically suspend execution?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Yes, execution is necessarily suspended. Article 285 of the Criminal Procedure Law prohibits execution until: The Federal Court of Cassation has finally affirmed the judgment. The papers have been sent through the Ministry of Justice to the Presidency for a presidential execution decree. While the challenge is pending or no decree has issued, execution is legally suspended.

294I received life imprisonment and believe the act was legally misclassified. What does a lawyer review?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Review intent to kill and criminal intent: Was there intent to take life and premeditation under Article 406 of the Penal Code? Was it an immediate fight without premeditation under Article 405? Was it an assault not intended to cause death but resulting in death under Article 410? A successful change in classification reduces life imprisonment to a fixed term of years.

295I received a long sentence and believe my actual role was overlooked. Which points require review?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Review criminal participation under Articles 47, 48 and 49 of the Penal Code: Establish that you did not carry out the material act. Establish the absence of prior agreement or effective assistance at the scene. Argue that your presence was incidental or limited to later transport without knowledge of the offence, seeking acquittal or reduction to the limits of secondary participation.

296Co-defendants received different sentences. How do we assess whether the difference is lawful or challengeable?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Lawful reasons include different roles, such as principal offender, instigator or facilitator, or personal circumstances, such as youth or a settlement by the victim's family with one defendant only. Ground for challenge: Where positions and evidence are identical but the court favours one without a reasoned judicial basis, the disparity is an injustice and a defect requiring quashing.

297I was convicted while another defendant was acquitted on similar evidence. Can this matter in cassation?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Yes, significantly. Challenge it as defective inference and inconsistency in the court's assessment. Where evidence is the same and shared, such as identical witness statements or seizure records, it cannot rationally or legally be accepted against one defendant and rejected for another without a logical justification recorded in the judgment.

298I believe the court applied a provision that does not fit the facts. What does a lawyer need?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

The judgment, charge sheet and facts established at trial are needed. The lawyer analyses the provision's elements and shows whether its material or mental conditions are absent from the facts, for example applying terrorism provisions to an ordinary tribal or criminal dispute, or the reverse.

299The court refused a defence witness or requested expert examination. When can this be raised after judgment?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Raise it directly in the cassation petition. The lawyer must establish that the witness or expert request was formally made at trial and refused or ignored, denying defence rights and preventing the truth from emerging.

300The judgment does not explain why some evidence was accepted and other evidence rejected. How is the reasoning reviewed?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Review under Article 224 of the Criminal Procedure Law, which requires reasoned judgments. Failure to explain logically the rejection of defence evidence and acceptance of weak evidence creates inadequate reasoning, among the common grounds for quashing before the Court of Cassation.

301The prosecution or another party seeks a harsher sentence. How can the convicted person prepare a defence?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

The lawyer immediately obtains the prosecution's cassation petition from the Court of Cassation. Prepare a responsive defence addressing the aggravation grounds and establishing the sentence's proportionality or the mitigating circumstances relied upon within the trial court's discretion.

302I received a criminal judgment in absentia and was later arrested. What determines the review route?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Criminal Court judgment in absentia: Cassation is unnecessary because arrest immediately requires a fresh trial in the defendant's presence under Article 247 of the Criminal Procedure Law. Misdemeanour or infraction judgment in absentia: An objection under Article 243(A) must be brought within three months for a misdemeanour from service or arrest, and within thirty days for an infraction from service. The court orders detention, schedules the objection and retries the case before the same court under Article 244.

303I have several criminal judgments in different cases. How does a lawyer organise their review and deadlines?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Separate each case by file number, issuing court and cassation deadline. Once judgments are final, apply under Articles 142 and 143 of the Penal Code for merger of sentences and execution of the most severe sentence alone, or reduction of the aggregate imprisonment under the statutory rules.

304Cassation affirmed the judgment. What does a lawyer review to determine whether another legal route remains?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Review is confined to two exceptional routes: Correction of the cassation decision under Article 266 of the Criminal Procedure Law, within a mandatory 30 days of service or notification. Retrial under Articles 270–279 of the Criminal Procedure Law, without a time limit, where new evidence emerges or witnesses are convicted of perjury.

305Cassation quashed the judgment and returned the case. What does this mean for the next trial?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Meaning: A legal victory returning the case to its starting point and removing the conviction. Next trial: The Criminal Court sets a new hearing and must follow the Court of Cassation's instructions, reopen investigation and hear the defences.

306A new judgment followed remittal after quashing. How is compliance checked, and how can the new judgment be reviewed?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Compare the new judgment's reasoning with the quashing decision. If the trial court disregarded the cassation directions and maintained its position, the lawyer challenges again. The case must then go to the General Panel of the Federal Court of Cassation for a final binding determination under Article 262 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

307What is the difference between cassation, correction of a cassation decision and retrial?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Cassation: Against first-instance judgments within 30 days of issue. Correction: Against the Court of Cassation's own decisions within 30 days of issue or service in the event of a serious legal error. Retrial under Articles 270–279 of the Criminal Procedure Law: An application without a time limit after finality, whenever decisive exculpatory facts or evidence emerge or witnesses are convicted of perjury.

308A new witness, document or evidence emerged after judgment. What determines its use after affirmation?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

The exclusive statutory basis is Article 270(Fourth) of Criminal Procedure Law No. 23 of 1971: where facts emerge or documents are produced after judgment that were unknown at trial and would establish the convicted person's innocence. Essential condition: The evidence must be decisive and necessarily establish innocence, and must have been unknown and impossible to discover or produce at trial. Under Article 271, the application is submitted to the Chief Public Prosecutor with supporting documents.

309A witness recanted or a document underlying the judgment is alleged to be forged. What must be checked before choosing a route?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Rule: Mere recantation or a statement is insufficient. A final criminal judgment must convict the witness of perjury under Article 251 of the Penal Code or the forger under Article 289. Step: First bring the criminal complaint. After a final conviction, immediately seek retrial to overturn the death or prison sentence.

310The judgment is old, and the family does not know whether cassation occurred or affirmed it. How is the current stage established?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Under a power of attorney or court permission, the lawyer reviews: The issuing Criminal Court's records, including the cassation dispatch register. The Federal Court of Cassation database using the file number and court. The prison administration's official legal-status statement from the Iraqi Corrections Service, identifying the final legal position and any enforcement orders.

311The convicted person is in prison, and the family has only a short judgment extract. Which papers are needed for a serious death or life-sentence review?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

A brief summary is insufficient. Officially obtain: A certified complete judgment with reasons. Trial hearing minutes. The principal investigation papers, post-mortem or forensic medical report, incident diagram and arresting unit's statement.

312The family lives abroad, and a relative is convicted in Iraq. How do we arrange authority, communication and cassation follow-up?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

The easiest and fastest route: The lawyer visits the central prison, where an official power of attorney is executed and authenticated by the prison director or authorised deputy acting as a prison notary, without the family attending. Alternative: Where appointment is by the family, execute a barcoded electronic consular power of attorney at the Iraqi embassy abroad and send it to the lawyer to begin procedures.

313The judgment came from outside Baghdad or from the Kurdistan Region. What determines the competent review authority?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Federal governorates: Regardless of governorate, including Basra, Mosul, Kirkuk or Anbar, the exclusive cassation authority is the Federal Court of Cassation in Baghdad. Kurdistan Region: The exclusive cassation authority is the Kurdistan Region Court of Cassation in Erbil. Required: The Criminal Court's name, case number and date, and place of issue, to determine the filing authority and applicable challenge procedure.
