222I paid an intermediary for residence or an extension and later discovered that the stamp or document might be forged. What should I prepare for a lawyer to assess my position?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Danger: Possessing a forged stamp or visa exposes the victim to prosecution for "Uttering a Forged Official Instrument" (استعمال محرر مزور) under Article (298 read with Article 289 of the Penal Code) — a major felony carrying up to 15 years' imprisonment. Immediate Protection: Do not present the forged document to any authority. Hand over the dossier to legal counsel: WhatsApp logs, bank/exchange transfer slips, and communication history. Counsel immediately files a criminal complaint against the intermediary under Article (456 - Fraud) and Article (289 - Forgery) to establish your status as a victim of crime, pre-empting criminal liability.

223I am a foreign national who paid an agency for a job and residence in Iraq, but the job turned out not to exist. What documents are needed to assess a claim or complaint?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Required Evidence: Written employment/recruitment contracts, original cash payment receipts, wire transfer records, and promotional materials. 225I invested in a project promising fixed or guaranteed returns, but payments stopped. What determines whether this is an investment dispute or suspected fraud?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

The Legal Distinguishing Line: Commercial Dispute (Civil): If genuine operational activity occurred, supported by formal commercial ledgers, but was afflicted by market downturns, the remedy is a civil action for accounting/capital recovery before the Commercial Court. Criminal Fraud (Penal Code Art. 456): If the entity was fictitious, promised impossible "guaranteed" high yields, operated a classic Ponzi scheme, or concealed insolvency, it constitutes criminal fraud. Counsel moves to freeze corporate bank accounts and petition for asset seizure.

237I was asked to receive money into my account and forward it to someone else for a commission, and now face a complaint. What information should I provide to a defence lawyer?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Extreme Statutory Danger: You have been utilized as a "Money Mule", which triggers charges of primary complicity in Money Laundering under Law No. 39 of 2015. Defense Strategy: Immediately assemble all recruitment communications proving complete absence of criminal intent (القصد الجنائي), lack of knowledge of the illicit origin of the funds, and nominal commission earnings. 241The other party accuses me of fraud because a contract was delayed or a project lost money, while I consider it a commercial dispute. What documents and facts would a lawyer need to assess my defence?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Strategic Defense: Opposing parties routinely attempt to criminalize commercial disputes to exert coercive pressure under Article (456). Required Defense Evidence: The underlying commercial contract and official corporate ledgers establishing genuine business execution. Correspondence, minutes of meetings, and external reports establishing that delays or losses stemmed from market fluctuations, force majeure, or the complainant's own breach. Counsel enters a decisive procedural motion before the investigating judge establishing the purely civil/commercial nature of the dispute, moving for immediate dismissal of criminal charges and closure of the investigation under Article (130/b) of the Criminal Procedure Code.
