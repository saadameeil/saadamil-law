329My personal or corporate bank account in Iraq was suddenly frozen. What is the first legal step to identify the cause and lift it?
Direct answer

Enquire formally through counsel with the bank's legal and compliance department to obtain the freeze order's reference number, date and issuing body (Economic Crime Investigation Judge, AML Office, Enforcement Directorate or Tax Authority), then submit a documented release application proving lawful transaction sources.

Legal reference

Anti-Money Laundering Law No. 39 of 2015 and Banking Law No. 94 of 2004.

Required documents

Official power of attorney, bank freeze notification, 12-month bank statement, and corporate incorporation or chamber-of-commerce credentials.

330Which documents prove lawful source of funds before the Economic Crime Investigation Court and the AML Office?
Direct answer

Every deposit or wire transfer must be matched to its underlying lawful transaction: authenticated supply or sale contracts, commercial invoices, customs import declarations and certificates of origin, Tapu real-estate sale deeds, and audited financial statements with tax clearance.

Legal reference

Anti-Money Laundering Law No. 39 of 2015 and Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979.

Required documents

Commercial or property sale contracts matching deposits, customs declarations, tax clearance certificates and sequential bank statements.

331The Investigation Judge refused to unfreeze our bank account. Can that refusal be appealed by cassation?
Direct answer

Yes. An Investigation Judge's refusal to lift a financial freeze may be challenged within 30 days before the Criminal Court acting in its cassation capacity under Articles 249 and 265 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

Legal reference

Articles 249 and 265 of Criminal Procedure Law No. 23 of 1971.

Required documents

Copy of the judicial refusal order, reasoned cassation petition, and indexed portfolio of financial and commercial source documents.

332A licensed exchange company received a Central Bank fine or platform suspension. How is a legal challenge pursued?
Direct answer

Counsel audits the alleged compliance breach under Central Bank of Iraq Law No. 56 of 2004 and exchange-company directives, submitting a documented administrative grievance backed by internal audit and KYC records, and escalating to competent judicial review where warranted.

Legal reference

Central Bank of Iraq Law No. 56 of 2004 and exchange-company regulatory instructions.

Required documents

Central Bank sanction or suspension letter, company licence, KYC compliance files and transaction ledgers under audit.

333How does an Iraqi importer protect funds in a Documentary Credit (LC) dispute involving shipping fraud?
Direct answer

While letters of credit are independent from the underlying sale contract under Iraqi Commerce Law No. 30 of 1984, manifest fraud or forged shipping/inspection documents allow an importer to seek an urgent Commercial Court injunction restraining bank payment pending substantive litigation.

Legal reference

Iraqi Commerce Law No. 30 of 1984 and Article 151 of Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

LC opening agreement and terms, bill of lading, inspection certificates, and evidence establishing documentary forgery or fraud.

334I transferred commercial funds through an intermediary or digital asset transfer (USDT) for goods never delivered. What is the legal route?
Direct answer

Counsel evaluates whether the facts constitute civil/commercial breach of contract (recovery and damages before the Court of First Instance with precautionary attachment) or criminal fraud/breach of trust under Penal Code Articles 456 and 453, preserving blockchain transaction hashes (TXIDs) and electronic communications under Law No. 78 of 2012.

Legal reference

Penal Code Articles 453 and 456, Evidence Law, and Electronic Transactions Law No. 78 of 2012.

Required documents

Transaction hash (TXID), cash handover receipts if any, unedited electronic chat logs and full identity particulars of the intermediary.

335When can a creditor obtain urgent precautionary attachment over a debtor's properties and bank accounts before filing suit?
Direct answer

Under Civil Procedure Law Article 231, a creditor holding an official or ordinary written instrument for a due, liquidated, unconditional debt may obtain precautionary attachment before or alongside filing suit upon providing security, provided the substantive debt and attachment-validation lawsuit is filed within 8 days.

Legal reference

Articles 231–240 of amended Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

Original debt instrument, contract or written acknowledgment, statutory security/bond, and particulars of the assets to be attached.

336Can a creditor attach payments owed to a debtor company by government ministries or banks (third-party garnishment)?
Direct answer

Yes. Both the Civil Procedure Law (Articles 233 et seq.) and Enforcement Law No. 45 of 1980 empower creditors to attach receivables, progress payments and deposits held for the debtor by state entities or banks, obliging the garnishee to declare and freeze the funds.

Legal reference

Articles 233 et seq. of the Civil Procedure Law and Enforcement Law No. 45 of 1980.

Required documents

Judicial attachment order or enforceable instrument, plus exact details of the ministry, company or bank holding the debtor's funds.

337I hold a high-value dishonoured cheque. Should I file a criminal complaint or enforce it directly at the Enforcement Directorate?
Direct answer

Pursuing both tracks simultaneously provides maximum leverage: a criminal complaint under Penal Code Article 459 triggers an arrest warrant, while lodging the cheque as a directly enforceable negotiable instrument under Enforcement Law Article 14 immediately attaches the drawer's properties and bank accounts without awaiting a civil judgment.

Legal reference

Penal Code Article 459 and Article 14 of Enforcement Law No. 45 of 1980.

Required documents

Original dishonoured cheque and certified copy, drawee bank's formal dishonour slip, and payee identification.

338What procedural mistakes weaken a dishonoured-cheque prosecution or commercial recovery?
Direct answer

While liability under Penal Code Article 459 arises upon issuing an unfunded cheque into circulation, failing to obtain a timely stamped bank dishonour notice within commercial limitation windows under Commerce Law No. 30 of 1984 can jeopardize negotiable-instrument remedies; prompt bank presentment is essential.

Legal reference

Penal Code Article 459 and commercial-paper limitation rules in Commerce Law No. 30 of 1984.

Required documents

Original cheque, bank presentment and dishonour stamp with date, and any underlying commercial contract.

339I signed a blank promissory note as security, and the holder filled in an inflated sum contrary to our agreement. How do I defend?
Direct answer

Abusing a blank signature contrary to an underlying security agreement triggers breach-of-trust or material-falsification defences depending on how the instrument was obtained. Defence relies on written proof of the underlying collateral contract, prior payment receipts and an application to stay enforcement.

Legal reference

Penal Code Article 453, Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979 and Commerce Law No. 30 of 1984.

Required documents

Copy of the enforced note, written contract showing collateral purpose, and receipts proving partial or full repayment.

340When can a travel ban be issued against a defaulting debtor or company managing director in Iraq?
Direct answer

A Court of First Instance judge or Judicial Executor may issue an urgent travel ban upon a creditor's application where a documented debt exists and serious grounds indicate risk of flight abroad to evade payment, notifying Passport and Border Crossings Directorates immediately.

Legal reference

Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969 and Enforcement Law No. 45 of 1980.

Required documents

Enforceable instrument or debt suit filings, plus the debtor's full four-part name, birth year and ID/passport details.

341Do debts of an Iraqi Limited Liability Company (LLC) extend to the personal assets of shareholders or the managing director?
Direct answer

Under amended Companies Law No. 21 of 1997, an LLC has separate legal personality and shareholder liability is limited to subscribed capital. However, personal liability attaches where a managing director commits fraud, signs an unfunded cheque incurring criminal liability under Article 459, or commingles corporate and personal funds.

Legal reference

Amended Iraqi Companies Law No. 21 of 1997 and Penal Code Article 459.

Required documents

Company certificate of incorporation, articles of association, the disputed instrument or cheque, and company financial statements.

342When can a creditor petition for commercial bankruptcy against a defaulting trader or company in Iraq?
Direct answer

A creditor holding a due, undisputed commercial debt may petition the competent Court of First Instance to declare a trader or commercial company bankrupt upon cessation of commercial payments reflecting credit collapse. The court appoints a bankruptcy trustee to inventory assets and claw back suspect transfers.

Legal reference

Applicable commercial bankruptcy and preventive composition provisions under Iraqi commercial legislation.

Required documents

Commercial debt instruments, formal non-payment protests, proof of the debtor's commercial registration, and schedule of unpaid debts.

343Can a foreign court money judgment or international arbitral award be enforced against a company's assets in Iraq?
Direct answer

Yes. Foreign judgments are enforced through exequatur proceedings before the Iraqi Court of First Instance under Foreign Judgments Enforcement Law No. 30 of 1928 and the Riyadh Judicial Cooperation Convention, while international arbitral awards are enforceable under the 1958 New York Convention ratified by Iraq via Law No. 14 of 2021.

Legal reference

Foreign Judgments Enforcement Law No. 30 of 1928, Riyadh Convention, and Law No. 14 of 2021 ratifying the New York Convention.

Required documents

Consular- and MOFA-authenticated original judgment or arbitral award, finality certificate, arbitration agreement, and certified Arabic translation.
