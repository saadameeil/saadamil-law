Corporate and investment legal questions

Procedural answers based on the Iraqi Companies Law and Investment Law:

What is required to establish a limited company or one-person company under Law No. 17 of 2019?

Under Iraqi Companies Law No. 21 of 1997, amended by Law No. 17 of 2019 (Iraqi Official Gazette issue 4554), a one-person limited company may be established without a nominal partner.

Procedures include reserving the trade name with the Chamber of Commerce, preparing and certifying the incorporation agreement, depositing the statutory capital in an approved bank, and appointing a managing director, certified accountant and permanent legal adviser with the Registrar of Companies.

Source: Companies Law No. 21 of 1997, amended by Law No. 17 of 2019
How is a branch of an Arab or international company registered in Iraq with the Companies Registration Directorate?

Registering a foreign company branch requires a legal file duly certified by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Iraqi embassy in the country of origin. It includes the incorporation agreement, registration certificate, approved accounts for the latest financial year, a resolution to open the Iraqi branch and appointment of a resident managing director.

The accepted statutory capital is deposited and security and sector approvals are pursued until the formal operating licence is issued.

Source: Instructions No. 2 of 2017 on registration of foreign company branches and offices
When can company debts affect the managing director's personal assets?

A company's assets are generally entirely separate from those of its managing director and partners. However, Companies Law Articles 118 and 123 impose personal and joint liability on the managing director's own assets in the following cases:

1. Acting outside the powers set out in the incorporation agreement and general assembly minutes.
2. Fraud, deception or dissipation of company funds and assets.
3. Continuing business and incurring debts after losses reach 75% of capital without calling the general assembly to liquidate or address the losses.

Source: Articles 118–123 of amended Companies Law No. 21 of 1997

How can partners and foreign investors protect their money in Iraqi partnership agreements?

A lawyer should review and approve essential preventive provisions in the incorporation agreement and supplementary agreements:

1. Considering a properly drafted commercial arbitration clause as an alternative for resolving disputes and reducing court litigation time.
2. Defining joint-signature controls for accounts and financial transactions above an agreed threshold.
3. Establishing a clear contractual mechanism for ending the partnership and valuing shares through legally accredited accounting audit firms.

Source: Commercial arbitration legislation and Iraqi Companies Law
My business partner took over management and refuses to distribute profits. How can I recover my entitlement?

We bring an accounting and profit payment claim before the Court of First Instance with commercial jurisdiction.

The court appoints a certified accounting expert to examine commercial books, bank accounts and contracts, and orders the controlling partner to pay the full profit share with compensation for delay and statutory interest.

Source: Civil Procedure Law Article 140 + Companies Law
What is required to liquidate a company and remove it from the commercial register without tax penalties?

Liquidation begins with a general assembly resolution and appointment of a legal liquidator, who publishes notice in the official bulletin and two daily newspapers. Debts and creditor claims are settled, final clearances are obtained from the General Commission for Taxes and the Workers' Retirement and Social Security Directorate, and the liquidator's final account is submitted to the Registrar of Companies for final removal from the register.

Source: Company liquidation provisions of amended Companies Law No. 21 of 1997
