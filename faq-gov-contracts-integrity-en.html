344A ministry instructed the bank to liquidate our performance guarantee while the contract dispute is ongoing. How do we stop liquidation?
Direct answer

Counsel files an urgent interim relief application before the competent Commercial Court or Court of First Instance under Civil Procedure Law Articles 150–151 to stay guarantee liquidation and notify the issuing bank immediately where the call is abusive or delay resulted from the ministry's own breach.

Legal reference

Government Contracts Implementation Instructions No. 2 of 2014 and Articles 150–151 of Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

Government contract, bank guarantee letter and extensions, ministry liquidation demand, and site stoppage or unpaid certificate records.

345What mandatory procedural steps must a state entity complete before issuing a withdrawal-of-work order?
Direct answer

Under Government Contracts Instructions No. 2 of 2014 and the General Conditions of Contract for Civil Engineering Works, the employer must first serve a formal written notice specifying the alleged defaults and granting the contractual cure period. A withdrawal order issued without valid notice or amidst employer-caused site obstructions is unlawful and subject to annulment and damages.

Legal reference

Government Contracts Implementation Instructions No. 2 of 2014 and Civil Engineering General Conditions of Contract.

Required documents

Withdrawal-of-work order, prior formal notice and service proof, project site diary, and official correspondence recording obstructions.

346Project site handover was delayed by encroachments and utility conflicts, yet delay fines were imposed. How are delay fines cancelled?
Direct answer

A contractor is not liable for delays caused by the employer, third-party site obstructions or force majeure. Counsel claims mandatory extension of time (EOT) and full waiver of delay penalties based on site-handover minutes, resident-engineer reports and timely notice letters.

Legal reference

Government Contracts Instructions No. 2 of 2014 and Articles 168 et seq. of the Iraqi Civil Code.

Required documents

Site handover minutes, resident engineer and utility-department letters confirming conflicts, and timely extension-of-time requests.

347When does the Baghdad Commercial Court have jurisdiction over government contract disputes versus the ordinary Court of First Instance?
Direct answer

Under Supreme Judicial Council directives establishing Commercial Courts, the Commercial Court exercises jurisdiction over commercial, investment and government contract disputes where at least one party is a foreign national or foreign company/branch. Disputes solely between Iraqi entities fall under the competent Court of First Instance unless a valid arbitration clause applies.

Legal reference

Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969 and Supreme Judicial Council directives on Commercial Court jurisdiction.

Required documents

Executed contract including dispute-resolution and forum clauses, plus corporate registration and nationality documents.

348The Ministry of Planning placed our contracting company on the Blacklist. How do we appeal and remove the debarment?
Direct answer

Counsel audits whether the recommending entity complied strictly with Ministry of Planning blacklisting regulations under Instructions No. 2 of 2014, filing a documented administrative grievance disproving fraud or wilful default, followed by judicial annulment proceedings.

Legal reference

Government Contracts Instructions No. 2 of 2014 and Ministry of Planning contractor blacklisting regulations.

Required documents

Blacklisting decision and service date, contracting entity correspondence, and technical/legal records refuting the alleged default.

349We completed a public project and obtained final acceptance, yet the state entity withholds final payments and cash retention. What is the remedy?
Direct answer

Once the maintenance period expires, final acceptance is issued and tax/social-security clearances are submitted, withholding retention money and final certificates has no lawful basis. We serve a notarial notice and file a monetary claim before the Commercial or First Instance Court including statutory interest under Civil Code Article 171.

Legal reference

Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951 (Article 171) and Government Contracts Instructions No. 2 of 2014.

Required documents

Provisional and final acceptance certificates, approved final measurement summary, and tax and social-security clearance certificates.

350I served on a public procurement or inspection committee and was summoned by the Integrity Commission under Article 340 or 331. What should I prepare?
Direct answer

Assemble the administrative order forming the committee and defining its remit, competitive price quotations or price-reasonableness minutes, spending-authority approvals, and any written dissent recorded, to establish procedural compliance and lack of criminal intent before giving statements.

Legal reference

Amended Integrity Commission Law No. 30 of 2011 and Penal Code Articles 331, 340 and 341.

Required documents

Committee formation order, procurement/inspection and price-moderation minutes, senior approvals, and internal or Financial Supervision Bureau audit reports.

351An administrative indemnification order for millions or billions of dinars was issued against me under Law No. 31 of 2015. Where and how do I appeal?
Direct answer

First file a mandatory written grievance with the issuing minister or head of non-ministerial entity within 30 days of service; upon actual or constructive rejection, file an annulment suit before the Administrative Court at the State Council, whose judgments are appealable to the Supreme Administrative Court.

Legal reference

Indemnification Law No. 31 of 2015, Instructions No. 2 of 2017, and amended State Council Law No. 65 of 1979.

Required documents

Indemnification decision and service proof, investigative committee report and damage valuation, and stamped copy of the timely administrative grievance.

352Can a state department enforce an indemnification order under the Government Debt Collection Law while our appeal is pending?
Direct answer

Where an entity initiates proceedings under Government Debt Collection Law No. 56 of 1977, counsel promptly files applications staying enforcement and contesting premature execution on the ground that the indemnification order has not satisfied statutory finality requirements under Law No. 31 of 2015.

Legal reference

Indemnification Law No. 31 of 2015 and Government Debt Collection Law No. 56 of 1977.

Required documents

Demand notice issued under Law No. 56 of 1977, and official receipt confirming filing of the Administrative Court challenge.

353What constitutes illicit enrichment (disproportionate wealth) under amended Integrity Commission Law No. 30 of 2011?
Direct answer

Illicit enrichment proceedings arise where an official subject to financial disclosure (or their spouse/children) shows substantial asset growth disproportionate to ordinary income without proven lawful source. Defence centres on a forensic accounting and legal portfolio documenting pre-office wealth, inheritances, lawful business income and historical property sales.

Legal reference

Integrity Commission and Illicit Enrichment Law No. 30 of 2011, as amended by Law No. 30 of 2019.

Required documents

Financial disclosure filings, historical Tapu deeds, inheritance certificates, prior sale contracts and historical bank statements.

354Can a private company's managing director be prosecuted before Integrity Courts for an ordinary breach of a state supply contract?
Direct answer

Mere delay or civil contractual non-performance is governed by contract terms and the Civil Code, not criminal law. Criminal jurisdiction before Integrity Courts attaches only where accompanied by forgery, bribery, intentional fraud in goods specifications, or collusion with public officials to harm public funds.

Legal reference

Amended Integrity Commission Law No. 30 of 2011 and Iraqi Penal Code No. 111 of 1969.

Required documents

State contract and addenda, technical inspection minutes, and official correspondence demonstrating good faith and civil dispute character.

355A public employee was suspended from duty (Sahb al-Yad) pending investigation. How are salary entitlements and reinstatement governed?
Direct answer

Articles 16–20 of amended State and Public Sector Employees Discipline Law No. 14 of 1991 govern suspension periods and half-salary payments during suspension or detention, entitling the employee to reinstatement and full payment of withheld salary halves upon acquittal, discharge or non-dismissal outcomes.

Legal reference

Articles 16–20 of amended State and Public Sector Employees Discipline Law No. 14 of 1991.

Required documents

Administrative suspension order, court acquittal/discharge or investigation closure decision, and formal grievance claiming withheld salary halves.

356Global material prices or official exchange rates shifted drastically during a government contract. Can the contractor claim relief for unforeseen hardship?
Direct answer

Under Article 146(2) of Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951 (doctrine of unforeseen exceptional events), where general, unforeseeable events render contractual performance excessively onerous and threaten exorbitant loss, the court may equitably restore balance and reduce the excessive burden.

Legal reference

Article 146(2) of amended Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

Executed contract and priced bill of quantities, official government indices/decisions proving the exceptional shift, and accounting records of exorbitant loss.

357A state entity instructed urgent variation works, took delivery and used them, then refused payment citing lack of a formal addendum. How do we recover?
Direct answer

Where additional works were instructed, measured by resident engineers, handed over and actually utilised by the public entity, the contractor claims payment under contract variation clauses or unjust-enrichment rules of Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Legal reference

Government Contracts Instructions No. 2 of 2014 and unjust-enrichment provisions of Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

Written site instructions, measurement sheets signed by the resident engineer, and project handover certificates.

358Our government or commercial contract includes an arbitration clause. Do Iraqi courts enforce arbitration agreements?
Direct answer

Yes. Under Civil Procedure Law Article 253, Iraqi courts stay litigation if a party invokes a valid arbitration clause at the first hearing, and Iraq enforces international arbitral awards under the 1958 New York Convention (Law No. 14 of 2021), subject to public-contract approval rules in Instructions No. 2 of 2014.

Legal reference

Articles 251–276 of the Civil Procedure Law, Law No. 14 of 2021, and Government Contracts Instructions No. 2 of 2014.

Required documents

Executed contract containing the arbitration clause, formal notice of dispute, and ministerial approvals applicable to public-sector arbitration.
