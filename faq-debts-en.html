94Someone borrowed money from me and denies the debt. What do I need to assess a claim?
Direct answer

A debt exceeding IQD 5,000 requires written evidence, a formal admission or an initial written evidentiary basis. If no written instrument exists at all, the lawyer requests a decisive oath from the denying debtor before the judge to determine the case conclusively.

Legal reference

Articles 77 and 108 of Iraqi Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979.

Required documents

The written instrument or receipt, transfer statements or conversations, and a request for a legally and religiously valid decisive oath.

95I have a debt claim without a receipt or written contract. Which evidence can a lawyer review?
Direct answer

Electronic correspondence and WhatsApp conversations, bank or exchange-office transfer records, witness testimony where there is a moral impediment to obtaining writing, such as a family relationship, or proof of a material fact, and a decisive oath directed to the opposing party.

Legal reference

Articles 11, 78 and 108 of Iraqi Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979.

Required documents

Transfer evidence, messages, witnesses within the limits of the moral impediment, and a decisive oath.

96I obtained a money judgment, but the debtor has not paid. What is the next step?
Direct answer

Obtain the enforceable form of the judgment and lodge it with the competent Enforcement Directorate, which serves a notice requiring payment within seven days. Pursue attachment of assets, property and wages, a travel restriction and ultimately enforcement detention.

Legal reference

Articles 40 onwards of the amended Iraqi Enforcement Law No. 45 of 1980.

Required documents

The original judgment bearing the enforcement formula and official details of the debtor's assets and addresses.

97The debtor wants instalments. What should be reviewed before agreeing to a settlement?
Direct answer

Check the debtor's solvency, specify instalment amounts and payment dates precisely, and include an express acceleration clause making the full balance immediately enforceable if one instalment is missed. Obtain a solvent guarantor or property mortgage as security.

Legal reference

Article 48 of Iraqi Enforcement Law No. 45 of 1980.

Required documents

A settlement instrument authenticated before the enforcement officer or notary public and security from a solvent guarantor.

98I received an Enforcement Directorate notice. What should I show a lawyer?
Direct answer

Review the enforcement file immediately within the notice's seven-day period to present legal defences, establish prior payment, challenge the enforceable judgment or offer an acceptable settlement, avoiding enforcement detention or attachments.

Legal reference

Articles 43–52 of Iraqi Enforcement Law No. 45 of 1980.

Required documents

The enforcement notice, file number, debt-payment receipts or grounds for disputing enforcement.

99Property belonging to me, or which I believe is not the debtor's, was attached. Which documents are needed to object?
Direct answer

Bring a third-party ownership claim before the Court of First Instance against the attaching creditor and debtor to lift the attachment and recover the property. Seek an urgent order temporarily suspending its sale by the Enforcement Directorate until ownership is determined.

Legal reference

Article 64 of Enforcement Law No. 45 of 1980 and Article 151 of the Civil Procedure Law.

Required documents

Title documents and invoices establishing the objector's ownership and possession before the attachment.

100I fear the debtor will dispose of assets before I recover my money. Which measures can be considered?
Direct answer

Apply urgently to the Court of First Instance judge for precautionary attachment of the debtor's movable and immovable assets and bank accounts under the Civil Procedure Law, preventing asset dissipation. Secure attachment before or alongside the substantive claim.

Legal reference

Articles 231–250 of the amended Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

The debt instrument or documents supporting the apparent right, payment of the required security, and identification of the debtor's assets and banks.

101The debt is old. Which dates and events determine whether I can still claim it?
Direct answer

Establish the due date. Ordinary debts become time-barred after 15 years, traders' and supply debts after one year, and independent professionals' claims after five years, provided limitation has not been interrupted by the debtor's acknowledgement or a court or enforcement claim.

Legal reference

Articles 399–443 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The date the debt arose and the last payment or correspondence acknowledging it and interrupting the limitation period.

102We want a financial settlement. Which clauses clarify and establish the obligations?
Direct answer

State the total amount in figures and words, instalment dates, acceleration of the entire debt on default and a release conditional on full payment. Authenticate the settlement before a notary public or record it in court minutes so it becomes an enforceable instrument.

Legal reference

Articles 705 onwards of the Civil Code and Article 48 of the Enforcement Law.

Required documents

A legally drafted settlement, the parties' identity documents and official notarial authentication to secure enforceability.

103I signed as someone's guarantor. What determines the limits of my liability?
Direct answer

The guarantee's wording governs. A joint and several guarantee allows the creditor to claim directly against you without first pursuing the debtor. A non-joint guarantee allows you to require prior enforcement against the debtor's assets. You retain recourse against the debtor for amounts paid on their behalf.

Legal reference

Articles 1008–1047 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The original guarantee, the demand addressed to the guarantor and evidence of the principal debtor's assets for prior enforcement.

141The other party denies signing the debt instrument. What is needed to assess the signature dispute?
Direct answer

Denial temporarily removes the private instrument's evidentiary force. Request handwriting comparison and specimens, and refer the document to criminal-evidence handwriting specialists for comparison with the opponent's earlier official documents. If genuine, the denier is fined and the debt awarded; if forged, the claim is dismissed.

Legal reference

Articles 39–44 of Iraqi Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979.

Required documents

The original debt instrument, the debtor's official signature samples from a notary public or banks, and a handwriting-specimen request.

142I signed a blank paper or receipt, and we now dispute what was written on it. Which facts and evidence are needed?
Direct answer

A signature on a blank document legally authorises completion unless the signer establishes breach of trust or theft of the paper. Prove this through messages and witnesses, and bring a criminal complaint under Article 454 of the Penal Code for misuse of a signed blank paper to suspend the civil claim.

Legal reference

Article 42 of Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979 and Article 454 of the Penal Code.

Required documents

The original instrument, correspondence establishing delivery while blank and its true purpose, and witness details.

143I paid a deposit, and we now dispute completing the sale or refunding it. What determines the position?
Direct answer

Under Iraqi law, a deposit ordinarily confirms a binding contract unless expressly agreed to be consideration for withdrawal. Where a withdrawal option is agreed, a withdrawing buyer loses the deposit, while a withdrawing seller must immediately return twice its value.

Legal reference

Article 92 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The deposit receipt and terms, and proof of withdrawal or refusal through an official notarial notice.

145I gave a cheque as security, and there is a dispute over presenting it. How do the agreement and correspondence affect the case?
Direct answer

Criminally, a cheque is a payment instrument rather than security, and the drawer is punishable under Article 459 upon refusal of payment. Civilly, the lawyer may use the written contract and correspondence to establish that it was entrusted as security, and seek its return and prevention of recovery where the entitlement has not arisen.

Legal reference

Article 459 of the Penal Code, Commerce Law No. 30 of 1984 and civil cassation principles.

Required documents

The original contract identifying the cheque as security, WhatsApp messages or emails, and an urgent action for return of the cheque.
