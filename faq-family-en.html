21I want to end my marriage. Which legal options may apply?
Direct answer

The legal options are: 1. A consensual khulʿ divorce for agreed consideration under Article 46; 2. A divorce pronounced unilaterally by the husband and recorded by the court under Article 34; 3. Judicial separation for harm, abandonment or irreconcilable conflict under Articles 40, 41 and 42.

Legal reference

The amended Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959.

Required documents

The official marriage contract, a statement explaining the conflict or harm, civil-register information and witness details.

22I have a marriage contract made outside court. What is needed to assess whether it can be authenticated?
Direct answer

Apply to the Personal Status Court to authenticate the marriage concluded outside court, undergo the medical examination, establish both spouses' legal age and consent, and present the two marriage witnesses so the court can issue an authentication certificate and register the marriage in the civil register.

Legal reference

Article 10(5) of the amended Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959.

Required documents

The informal marriage contract, if available; both spouses' national ID cards; the medical examination; and the children's birth information for establishing parentage.

23I want to claim my deferred dower. What determines my entitlement and the procedure?
Direct answer

Deferred dower is a preferential debt owed by the husband, payable upon the earlier of death or an irrevocable divorce or judicial separation. A claim is brought before the Personal Status Court, and its value is assessed under the marriage contract, with adjustment for currency depreciation where required.

Legal reference

Articles 19 and 20 of Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959.

Required documents

The official marriage contract, a final divorce or judicial separation judgment, or the husband's death certificate.

24A husband is not supporting his wife. What information is needed to assess a maintenance claim?
Direct answer

The wife is entitled to maintenance from the date a husband with sufficient means stops providing it, unless she has been declared disobedient by a court judgment. A claim for ongoing and past spousal maintenance is brought, with an assessment based on the husband's employment or self-employment income and property.

Legal reference

Articles 23–27 of Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959.

Required documents

The marriage contract, evidence of the husband's means such as salary confirmation, a commercial register or property ownership, and confirmation that no final judgment of marital disobedience exists.

25A father is not supporting his children. How can their mother begin claiming their rights?
Direct answer

Bring a claim for ongoing and past child maintenance before the Personal Status Court. The lawyer may request urgent interim maintenance at the first hearing pending determination of the case, establishment of the father's income and enforcement of payment.

Legal reference

Article 59 of the Personal Status Law and Article 302 of the Civil Procedure Law.

Required documents

The children's birth records, the mother's and children's national ID cards, and evidence of the father's employment or assets.

26There is a dispute over child custody. Which facts should be presented to the lawyer?
Direct answer

The children's ages; the proposed custodian's ability, trustworthiness and suitability to raise and care for them; whether she has married a man who is not a prohibited-degree relative of the child; and the child's best interests as the governing criterion for the Personal Status Court judge.

Legal reference

Article 57 of the amended Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959.

Required documents

The children's birth certificates, medical or psychological reports where there is a dispute, and evidence supporting good conduct.

27The other parent prevents me from seeing my children. Which legal options need to be considered?
Direct answer

Bring a claim before the Personal Status Court to determine visitation, taking the children out and overnight contact. After judgment, lodge it with the Enforcement Directorate for compulsory implementation; a person who refuses compliance faces fines and judicial proceedings.

Legal reference

Article 57(4) of Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959.

Required documents

The divorce judgment or evidence of separation, the children's identity documents, and a suitable proposed time and place for contact.

28I want to travel with my child, but the other parent objects. Which permissions or orders should be reviewed?
Direct answer

A custodian may not legally take the child outside Iraq without the father's written consent as legal guardian, or a specific, reasoned permission from the Personal Status Court judge with adequate safeguards for the child's return.

Legal reference

Article 57(5) of the Personal Status Law and established principles of the Federal Court of Cassation.

Required documents

The child's and custodian's passports, the custody certificate and an application for judicial travel permission.

29My child is not officially registered. What is needed to determine the parentage and registration procedure?
Direct answer

Bring a parentage action before the Personal Status Court, refer the child and both parents to the Forensic Medicine Institute for DNA matching, and then require the Civil Status Directorate to issue the birth certificate and civil-register entry.

Legal reference

Articles 51 and 52 of Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959.

Required documents

The marriage contract made outside court or evidence of a lawful marital relationship, a hospital or licensed midwife's birth confirmation, and the forensic DNA test.

30My marriage contract or divorce judgment is lost. How can I obtain an official copy?
Direct answer

Apply to the head of the Personal Status Court that issued the document for a certified copy from the permanent records, after identifying the file number and year of issue and paying the statutory stamp fee.

Legal reference

The Judicial Fees Law and Iraqi Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979.

Required documents

An approximate marriage or divorce date, the parties' full names and a copy of the applicant's national ID card.

31A maintenance order exists, but living circumstances have changed. What is needed to assess an increase or reduction?
Direct answer

Bring a maintenance variation claim after one full year from the previous judgment, or when a substantial change occurs in the liable person's income, such as a salary increase or insolvency, or in the recipient's educational and healthcare needs.

Legal reference

Article 28 of the amended Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959.

Required documents

The previous maintenance judgment, evidence of changes in the payer's income and evidence of new needs and expenses.

32A family member has died. Which documents are needed for an inheritance certificate?
Direct answer

Obtain a Sharia or civil inheritance certificate from the Personal Status Court according to the deceased's school of law, Jaʿfari or Hanafi, to identify the lawful heirs, determine each share and exclude those who do not inherit under the law.

Legal reference

Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959 and the Jaʿfari Personal Status Code published in Iraqi Gazette issue 4843.

Required documents

The original death certificate, the 1957 family civil-register extract, the heirs' identity documents and two informed witnesses attending court.

33An heir was omitted from the inheritance certificate. What should a lawyer review?
Direct answer

Bring an action before the Personal Status Court to invalidate and correct the inheritance certificate, include the omitted heir and redistribute the estate. Request invalidation of financial transactions, dispositions and sales based on the invalid certificate.

Legal reference

Article 1070 of the Civil Code and Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

The incorrect inheritance certificate, a civil-register extract establishing the omitted heir's relationship to the deceased, and the corrective inheritance certificate.

34The deceased left both debts and assets. What must be established before distributing the estate?
Direct answer

Apply the governing legal and Sharia rule that no estate is distributable until debts have been paid. Funeral preparation and burial expenses are paid first, then established creditors' debts, then the will is implemented within the one-third limit; the remainder is distributed among the heirs.

Legal reference

Articles 1100 and 1104 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The inheritance certificate, an inventory of the estate and official debt instruments or enforceable judgments against the deceased.

35A minor is among the heirs. What legal matters must be reviewed before dealing with the child's share?
Direct answer

The Minors Care Directorate must be notified. A minor's share may not legally be sold, mortgaged or relinquished without permission and approval from that directorate and the competent Investigation or Personal Status Court judge, after establishing the transaction's benefit and exclusive advantage to the child.

Legal reference

Articles 43 onwards of Minors Care Law No. 78 of 1980.

Required documents

The inheritance certificate, a guardianship certificate, a property valuation and fair-market-price assessment, and the Minors Care Directorate's official approval.

131I received a decision changing the rules applied to my marriage contract. Which papers and dates should a lawyer review?
Direct answer

Review the decision and formal service dates to calculate the mandatory 30-day challenge period, and check whether the original marriage contract specified a particular school of law. Challenge before the Federal Court of Cassation to reverse a change made without both spouses' consent or contrary to procedural rules.

Legal reference

Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959 and Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

The challenged decision, original marriage contract, certificate identifying the school of law and formal service date.

132I want to claim the marital home's furniture. What establishes ownership of the items?
Direct answer

Ownership may be proved by a furniture list signed by the husband or his guarantor, whether private or official, purchase invoices, or witnesses establishing that the wife bought and supplied it with her own funds. If written evidence is absent, a decisive oath is directed to the denying husband.

Legal reference

Articles 19, 20 and 51 of the Personal Status Law and Articles 77 and 108 of Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979.

Required documents

The signed furniture list, appliance purchase receipts, marriage contract, witness evidence or an inventory of the items.

133The other parent retains my children's official documents and refuses to return them. Which options need consideration?
Direct answer

Apply to the Personal Status Court judge for an order on petition requiring immediate delivery under Article 151 of the Civil Procedure Law, as identity documents may not be withheld. Alternatively bring a delivery action and enforce it compulsorily through the Enforcement Directorate.

Legal reference

Articles 151 and 152 of Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969 and the Minors Care Law.

Required documents

The custody certificate or proof of guardianship, the children's national ID numbers and a formal delivery demand.

134An heir produced a will whose validity we dispute. Which documents and facts are needed?
Direct answer

Check the will's formal status, whether authenticated by a notary public or court or privately made, the testator's mental capacity when signing, and enforceability within one-third of the estate. Any excess depends on the other heirs' approval, with a right to challenge forgery or invalidity in court.

Legal reference

Articles 64–74 of Personal Status Law No. 188 of 1959 and Article 1109 of the Civil Code.

Required documents

The original will, the testator's medical reports before death, the inheritance certificate and an estate inventory.

135My father transferred property before his death, and the heirs question the transaction. What needs checking?
Direct answer

Check the transfer date. A transaction during terminal illness is treated as a will and is ineffective beyond one-third of the estate without the heirs' approval. Alternatively, bring an action before the Court of First Instance to invalidate a sham sale by establishing non-payment, unfair undervalue or collusion to deprive other heirs.

Legal reference

Articles 148 and 1109 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The previous and current title documents, the deceased's pre-death medical reports and evidence of the sham transaction and non-receipt of payment.
