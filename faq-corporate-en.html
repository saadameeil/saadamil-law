66I want to start a business in Iraq. What determines the appropriate legal form?
Direct answer

The capital, number of partners and financial risk determine the choice. A sole proprietorship entails unlimited personal liability. A limited liability company, or LLC, is the optimal secure option for protecting the partners' personal assets and limiting liability to their capital shares.

Legal reference

Articles 4–28 of the amended Iraqi Companies Law No. 21 of 1997.

Required documents

The proposed activity and trade name, copies of the founders' identity documents and an approved lease for the company's premises.

67Before signing a partnership agreement, which provisions should a lawyer review?
Direct answer

Capital-share percentages, allocation of profits and losses, management and financial representation powers, exit and withdrawal arrangements, asset valuation on liquidation, and dispute resolution through arbitration or the competent courts.

Legal reference

Companies Law No. 21 of 1997 and the Iraqi Civil Code.

Required documents

The draft partnership agreement, the feasibility study and a statement of each party's financial and operational obligations.

68My partner denies access to the accounts or my profits. Which documents are needed to assess my rights?
Direct answer

Send a notarial notice to the managing director and partner requiring the statements, then bring an accounting action and request a court-appointed accounting expert, appointment of a judicial receiver or determination of company profits, and an order requiring management to hand over the amounts due.

Legal reference

Articles 62 and 65 of the amended Companies Law No. 21 of 1997 and Article 158 of the Civil Procedure Law.

Required documents

The company's incorporation documents, registration certificate from the Companies Registrar and general assembly minutes.

69I paid a supplier or contractor who did not perform. Which options need to be considered?
Direct answer

Serve a notarial notice requiring completion within a specified period. If performance is refused, seek rescission, compensation for losses and lost profit, forfeiture of security and the guarantee letter, and application of the agreed delay penalties.

Legal reference

Articles 168, 170 and 255 of the Iraqi Civil Code No. 40 of 1951.

Required documents

The supply or works contract, specifications schedule, bank payment receipts and notifications, and the notarial notice.

70My company has unpaid invoices. What is needed to assess recovery?
Direct answer

Check the recipient's signature and stamp on delivery receipts and supply lists, serve a formal payment demand, and bring a commercial claim before the competent Court of First Instance with statutory late-payment interest of 5% in commercial matters.

Legal reference

Article 171 of Commerce Law No. 30 of 1984 and the Iraqi Evidence Law.

Required documents

The signed and stamped original invoices, purchase orders, delivery notices and demand letters.

71Someone is using my business name or brand. What determines whether it can be protected?
Direct answer

Registration with the Industrial Property Protection Section of the Ministry of Industry and Minerals is decisive. A registered trademark gives its owner the exclusive right to seek an injunction, compensation and seizure of counterfeit goods, alongside a criminal complaint.

Legal reference

Trademarks and Trade Descriptions Law No. 21 of 1957, as amended by Order No. 80 of 2004.

Required documents

The valid trademark registration certificate, samples of the original and counterfeit products, and details of where the counterfeit goods are sold.

72I want to buy a business or shares in an existing company. What legal due diligence is needed before payment?
Direct answer

Conduct due diligence by checking the Companies Registrar's records, tax and social-security clearances, bank debts, pending cases and disputes, and certified final accounts.

Legal reference

Companies Law No. 21 of 1997 and Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982.

Required documents

The company registration certificate, final accounts for the last three years, and tax and social-security clearance certificates.

73I want to leave a partnership or close a company. Which obligations should be reviewed?
Direct answer

Review restrictions in the incorporation documents on exit and offering shares first to the other partners, settle creditors' debts, obtain tax and social-security clearances, and have the general assembly resolve to dissolve the company and appoint an official liquidator, with approval by the Companies Registrar.

Legal reference

Articles 158–178 of the amended Iraqi Companies Law No. 21 of 1997.

Required documents

The general assembly minutes for withdrawal or dissolution, the company's financial position and clearances from all relevant authorities.

74A foreign company wants to operate in Iraq. What determines its legal requirements?
Direct answer

Determine whether to register a foreign-company branch or commercial representative office, provide consularly authenticated parent-company documents, appoint a resident branch manager and legal representative, open an Iraqi bank account and meet the branch-registration rules.

Legal reference

Regulation No. 2 of 2017 on Branches and Representative Offices of Foreign Companies and Institutions.

Required documents

The authenticated parent company's incorporation documents and registration certificate, the resolution opening the branch and designation of the Iraqi authorised manager.

75We want to change the manager, shareholders or capital. Which procedures and documents require review?
Direct answer

Properly convene the company's general assembly, adopt the decision by the required majority, record it in authenticated official minutes and submit it to the Companies Registration Department for publication in the special bulletin and entry in the company's registration certificate and records.

Legal reference

Articles 85–105 of the amended Companies Law No. 21 of 1997.

Required documents

The general assembly minutes, amended company documents and the new manager's or shareholders' national ID cards.

144My bank froze my account or refused a transaction. Which documents establish the reason and possible challenge?
Direct answer

Enquire officially whether the freeze results from an Investigation Court order, enforcement attachment or anti-money-laundering notification. Approach the Central Bank and judicial authority to establish the lawful financial position and obtain an order lifting the restriction and releasing the account.

Legal reference

Anti-Money Laundering Law No. 39 of 2015 and Banking Law No. 94 of 2004.

Required documents

The bank's refusal notice, customer identification and tax card, and evidence of lawful sources of funds.

151A former employee took company customer data or files for a competing business. Which evidence and contracts need review?
Direct answer

Review the employment contract, confidentiality and non-compete provisions. Bring a criminal complaint under Article 437 of the Penal Code for disclosure of secrets and Article 453 for breach of trust, and a commercial first-instance action for compensation, an injunction against unfair competition and return of databases.

Legal reference

Articles 437 and 453 of the Penal Code and Labour Law No. 37 of 2015.

Required documents

The confidentiality agreement, data-extraction evidence such as logs and emails, and evidence of unlawful contact with customers.

152A dispute arose under a contract containing an arbitration clause. What determines the forum?
Direct answer

An arbitration clause removes the ordinary courts' authority to determine the dispute if invoked at the first hearing before addressing the merits. Proceed promptly to constitute the tribunal and commence arbitration under the contract and Civil Procedure Law.

Legal reference

Articles 251–276 of the amended Civil Procedure Law No. 83 of 1969.

Required documents

The original contract with the arbitration clause, commencement notice and arbitrator appointments, and identification of the dispute.

153My company's shipment is held over documents or customs procedures. Which papers are needed?
Direct answer

Check the import licence, original invoice and certificate of origin, and whether detention concerns standards and quality control or reassessment of customs duty. Submit a grievance to the General Customs Directorate and challenge before the Customs Court to seek release against security.

Legal reference

The amended Iraqi Customs Law No. 23 of 1984.

Required documents

The bill of lading, certificate of origin, import licence, quality inspection report and customs detention letter.

154I invested money without clearly registering my share, and the other party denies the partnership. What evidence helps?
Direct answer

Prove payment through bank transfers or receipts and the actual partnership through accounting messages, earlier profit distributions and the opponent's admission. Bring an action establishing a de facto partnership and requiring return of capital and agreed profits, or a claim based on unjust enrichment.

Legal reference

Articles 626 and 627 of the Iraqi Civil Code and Evidence Law No. 107 of 1979.

Required documents

Bank-transfer statements, WhatsApp conversations, earlier profit distributions and witnesses to the commercial transaction.

155I want to terminate a commercial agency or distribution contract. What should be reviewed before notice?
Direct answer

Review termination conditions and the contractual notice period, and whether the agency is registered with the Companies Registration Department. Serve a notarial termination notice within the required period to avoid claims for abusive termination and the agent's lost profits.

Legal reference

Commercial Agency Regulation Law No. 79 of 2017 and the Iraqi Civil Code.

Required documents

The authenticated agency contract, commercial agency licence and official notarial advance notice.
