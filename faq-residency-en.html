104My Iraqi residence permission has expired or is about to expire. What information does a lawyer need?
Direct answer

The residence endorsement's expiry date and visa type, such as work, tourist or multiple-entry, are needed. Regularise the position promptly with the Directorate of Residency Affairs, pay the prescribed late fines and seek an extension to avoid detention or a deportation order.

Legal reference

Articles 11, 42 and 43 of Iraqi Foreigners Residency Law No. 76 of 2017.

Required documents

The original valid passport, residence endorsement and employment contract or guarantee from the sponsoring Iraqi entity.

105A foreign national wants to work in Iraq. What legal matters should be checked before starting?
Direct answer

Obtain an entry visa for work in advance, secure an official work permit from the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs, obtain Ministry of Interior security approval and undergo a medical examination for communicable diseases before taking any employment.

Legal reference

Article 30 of Labour Law No. 37 of 2015 and Foreigners Residency Law No. 76 of 2017.

Required documents

The passport, forensic medical examination certificate, Ministry of Labour work permit and employment contract.

106My company wants to hire foreign workers. Which obligations require review?
Direct answer

Strictly observe the national workforce ratio, with Iraqis making up at least 50% of company workers. Register foreign workers for social security, obtain work-residence cards, pay the prescribed recruitment fees and notify the authorities immediately when their contracts end.

Legal reference

Article 31 of Labour Law No. 37 of 2015 and Retirement and Social Security Law No. 18 of 2023.

Required documents

The company's worker register, official work permits and Ministry of Labour and Ministry of Interior approvals.

107A foreign worker changed employer. What legal issues affect the worker's status and residence?
Direct answer

Obtain written approval and clearance from the original employer or former sponsor, and formally transfer sponsorship with Directorate of Residency Affairs and Ministry of Labour approval. Otherwise the worker and new employer breach residence conditions and face penalties.

Legal reference

Foreigners Residency Law No. 76 of 2017 and applicable Ministry of Labour instructions.

Required documents

The former employer's release and clearance, the new sponsor's employment contract and the updated residence record.

108My residence or renewal application was refused. Which documents help assess the reason and options?
Direct answer

Identify whether the refusal is security-related, administrative or medical. Submit a formal grievance to the Minister of Interior and Director General of Residency within the statutory period, or remedy administrative omissions. Avoid unlawful residence to prevent detention and deportation.

Legal reference

Articles 16 onwards of Iraqi Foreigners Residency Law No. 76 of 2017.

Required documents

The official residence refusal letter, previous residence documents and the legal grievance submitted.

109I face an entry ban or deportation-related action. What information is needed to assess it?
Direct answer

Identify the entry-ban letter or deportation order number and the requesting authority, such as the judiciary, National Security Service or intelligence. Appoint an Iraqi lawyer to approach the Ministry of Interior's banned-persons section and seek removal of the entry where the grounds for deportation have ceased.

Legal reference

Articles 17–24 of Foreigners Residency Law No. 76 of 2017.

Required documents

A passport copy, the deportation letter's number and date, and documents refuting the grounds for the ban.

110I lost my passport inside Iraq. Which authorities and procedures must be identified?
Direct answer

Report the loss formally at a police station and have the report approved by the Investigation Court judge. Contact your embassy in Baghdad for a new passport or travel document, then approach the Directorate of Residency Affairs to transfer and record the residence endorsement.

Legal reference

The Criminal Procedure Law and Iraqi Foreigners Residency Law No. 76 of 2017.

Required documents

The judicial loss report, the embassy-issued replacement passport and confirmation of the previous residence record in the computer system.

165I changed my passport or personal details while resident in Iraq. What should be reviewed in my residence file?
Direct answer

Approach the Directorate of Residency Affairs within a maximum of 15 days after receiving the new passport to transfer the residence endorsement and entry information and update the Central Computer records, avoiding fines or irregular residence status.

Legal reference

Iraqi Foreigners Residency Law No. 76 of 2017 and its implementing instructions.

Required documents

The old and embassy-issued new passports, residence-address confirmation and the residence-data update form.

194I entered Iraq for a visit or a business meeting and then received a job offer. What should I check before starting work or changing the purpose of my stay?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Rule: Article (11) of Foreigners Residency Law No. 76 of 2017 strictly prohibits any foreign national who enters on an ordinary visit, tourist, or religious visa from engaging in any remunerated or unremunerated employment. Procedural Steps: Do not commence work under any circumstances prior to regularisation. Unauthorized work triggers immediate administrative fines, detention, deportation, and blacklisting, along with corporate penalties for the employer. The hiring entity must formally petition the Ministry of Labor and Social Affairs (MOLSA) to obtain an official Work Permit (إجازة عمل) and submit an application to the Directorate of Residency Affairs to convert your entry status pursuant to Ministerial approval.

195I am visiting Iraq and want to stay longer for a different purpose. Can I change my status, and what information and documents are needed to assess my situation?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Rule: Status conversion from a tourist/visit visa inside federal Iraq is an exceptional procedure under Article (11/Second) requiring substantiated statutory grounds (accredited employment, marriage to an Iraqi citizen, approved investment, or academic enrollment). Required Documents for Audit: Original passport valid for at least six months, entry visa stamp with border control entry slips, and verified documentation substantiating the new statutory ground (authenticated employment contract, certified marriage certificate, or university admission letter).

196I am a foreign national married to an Iraqi citizen. What is needed to assess whether I can obtain residence based on marriage?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Rule: Article (12) of Law No. 76 of 2017 grants foreign spouses of Iraqi nationals the statutory right to apply for renewable residency. Required Documents: An official marriage contract issued or ratified by a competent Iraqi Personal Status Court (محكمة الأحوال الشخصية); the Iraqi spouse’s Unified National Card (البطاقة الوطنية) or Civil ID and Nationality Certificate; certified residence confirmation (تأييد سكن); and a clean criminal record check. The Directorate of Residency Affairs issues a renewable annual spouse residency permit.

197I hold work-related residence in Iraq and want my spouse and children to live with me. What should we review regarding their residence and documents?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Rule: Foreigners holding valid work residency may sponsor immediate family members (spouse and minor children) as dependants under Article (12/Third). Required Documents: Marriage certificate and children’s birth certificates—duly apostilled/attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in the home country, the Iraqi Embassy abroad, and the Iraqi Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Baghdad; lease agreement; and proof of lawful financial solvency, accompanied by formal undertakings that dependants will not engage in employment without independent work permits.

198We are foreign parents and our child was born in Iraq. How can we arrange the birth documents and review our child’s residence status?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Rule: Birth on Iraqi soil does not automatically confer Iraqi nationality upon children of foreign parents (Jus Sanguinis principle applies under Iraqi Nationality Law No. 26 of 2006). Procedural Roadmap: Obtain the official Birth Notification (بيان ولادة) from the hospital and ratify it at the Health Directorate and Personal Status Court. Register the birth at your home country’s Embassy/Consulate in Baghdad to obtain a national passport or travel document for the newborn. Present the foreign passport to the Directorate of Residency Affairs to endorse the child’s residency permit linked to the parents' legal status.

199I am an international student accepted by an Iraqi university. What should I check about entry and residence before enrolling and arranging accommodation?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Rule: Academic residency requires an official Student Visa issued under Article (5) of Law No. 76 of 2017. Checklist: Ensure you possess an official admission letter validated by the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (Department of Scholarships and Cultural Relations). Upon arrival, submit the university sponsorship letter to Residency Affairs to obtain an annual student residency card. Employment on a student visa is statutorily prohibited.

200I want to invest in or become a shareholder of a business in Iraq. Is investment alone sufficient to obtain residence, or are separate procedures required?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Rule: Passive capital allocation or private share purchase does not confer automatic residency. Procedural Roadmap: Investor residency requires either: (a) An official Investment License issued by the National Investment Commission (NIC) or Provincial Investment Commissions under Investment Law No. 13 of 2006 (as amended); or (b) Incorporating an Iraqi corporate entity or registering a foreign branch with the Registrar of Companies (دائرة تسجيل الشركات) at the Ministry of Trade, appointing the foreign national as an authorized Managing Director (مدير مفوض) or qualified shareholder, which serves as the legal foundation for investor residency.

201The company connected to my residence closed or terminated my contract. How might this affect my residence, and what information is needed to assess my options?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Rule: Once an employment contract terminates or the sponsoring entity dissolves, the legal basis of your work residency lapses immediately, triggering the employer's statutory duty to notify Residency Affairs. Your Options: You have a grace window (ordinarily 30 days) to either: (a) Transfer your residency to a new, licensed corporate sponsor; or (b) Procure an official Exit Visa (سمة خروج) and depart the country to avoid compounding daily fines, arrest, and formal deportation orders.

202I hold Iraqi residence and plan to spend a long period abroad. What should I check before travelling regarding my residence status and ability to return?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Rule: Under Article (18) of Foreigners Residency Law No. 76 of 2017, if a foreign resident remains outside Iraq for a continuous period exceeding six (6) months, their residency permit is revoked by operation of law. Pre-Travel Verification: Ensure you return within the 6-month statutory limit, or obtain advance written authorization from the Minister of Interior or Director General of Residency Affairs establishing compelling justification prior to departure.

203My residence renewal is still pending and I need to travel urgently. What should I review before booking or leaving Iraq?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Warning: Departing Iraq while your residency card is expired and your renewal application is still in process will result in border interception at the airport, heavy statutory overstay fines, forfeiture of the pending renewal file, and possible re-entry denial. Procedural Relief: Petition Residency Affairs through your legal counsel to procure an Emergency Exit & Re-entry Visa (سمة خروج وعودة اضطرارية) or an authenticated facilitation letter verifying that your renewal dossier is actively pending.

204I entered Iraq through the Kurdistan Region or have a residence application there, and now want to live or work in Baghdad. What should be checked before I move?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Trap: Entry visas and residency permits issued exclusively by the Kurdistan Regional Government (KRG) are not valid for unmonitored work or permanent residence in federal Iraq (Baghdad and southern governorates) under federal immigration enforcement. Required Action: You must regularise your status with the Federal Directorate of Residency Affairs in Baghdad, obtain a federal security clearance, and secure an official federal entry visa/work residency to avoid detention at checkpoints or airport border terminals.

205I changed my address or moved to another Iraqi governorate. What should I review in my residence records and documents?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Mandate: Article (14) of Law No. 76 of 2017 obligates all foreign residents to inform the Directorate of Residency Affairs or the local police station of any change in residence within 48 hours of moving. Action Required: Secure a newly certified Residence Card (تأييد سكن) from the local Mukhtar endorsed by the District Commissioner (القائممقامية), and update your registration records at the Residency Affairs branch to prevent administrative penalties.

206My residence card or document was lost or damaged, but I still have my passport. What information and documents should I prepare for a lawyer?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Procedural Roadmap: Immediately lodge a formal police report (إخبار رسمي) regarding the loss/damage before the local investigative court to secure an official judicial order. Provide counsel with your original passport containing the entry stamp, previous residency record/number, and photographs. Counsel will petition the Directorate of Residency Affairs with the judicial order to issue an official replacement card (بدل ضائع/تالف).

207My residence file is in one governorate, but I am temporarily working in another. What determines where my application should be handled?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal Rule: Territorial jurisdiction is governed by the registered corporate domicile of the sponsoring company or the formally approved project site designated in the MOLSA Work Permit. Secondments or remote site allocations require authenticated inter-branch corporate delegation notices and coordinating letters between regional residency directorates to maintain lawful field mobility.

208An intermediary is holding my original residence documents and receipts without giving me a tracking or file reference. How can the application’s status be checked through official channels?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Violation: Withholding foreign travel documents and official receipts constitutes an unlawful act under Iraqi law. Remedy: Appointed counsel presents a formal Power of Attorney directly to the Directorate of Residency Affairs (Audit & Follow-up Division). By querying the central national database using your passport number, counsel can extract the official docket number and file status, followed by serving a formal legal notice or initiating criminal proceedings against the intermediary under Articles 453 (Breach of Trust) or 438 of the Penal Code.

209My residence is about to expire and I am in hospital or unable to attend in person. What would a lawyer need to assess how the matter can be handled?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Statutory Exception: Incapacitation due to serious medical emergencies constitutes force majeure, pausing statutory late-filing fines if officially certified. Required Documents: A comprehensive medical report issued by a licensed public/governmental hospital, authenticated by the competent Health Directorate; and a legalized Power of Attorney. Counsel petitions the Director General of Residency Affairs to stay administrative penalties and request an exceptional bedside consular delegation or an emergency procedural extension.
