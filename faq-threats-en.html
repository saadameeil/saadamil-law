242Someone threatens to kill or abduct me and knows my home and workplace. Which facts and evidence are needed urgently?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: A felony involving a threat to kill or abduct under Article 430 or 431 of the Penal Code. Immediate evidence: Recorded calls, screenshots of threatening messages, statements from witnesses who heard the threats, and home or workplace CCTV. Urgent action: File an immediate complaint with the Investigation Court judge, give a sworn statement and seek an immediate arrest warrant or summons, a protection order and a security patrol at your home and workplace.

243I receive WhatsApp or Telegram threats from changing, unknown numbers or accounts. What information should I organise?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Evidence: Keep original conversations without deleting or blocking, obtain the Telegram username and user ID, and capture screenshots showing the complete telephone number, date and time. Procedure: Complain to the Investigation Court judge and obtain an order contacting the Organised Crime Directorate's cybercrime section and telecommunications companies, Zain, Asiacell and Korek, to identify the subscriber and location through cell-tower or IP information.

244Someone has private photos or conversations and demands money not to publish them. What should I show a lawyer?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: A completed felony of financial blackmail with threats to disclose dishonourable matters under Article 430(1) of the Penal Code. Evidence: Threat and extortion messages, proof of the monetary demand and the requested wallet or account number, without deleting any message. Practical action: Complain and coordinate immediately with the National Security Service's anti-cyber-blackmail unit to apprehend the suspect in the act at collection or through a security operation.

245Someone threatens me to waive a debt or case or sign a document. How does coercion affect the waiver or signature?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal and civil classification: Physical or psychological coercion vitiating consent under Articles 112–116 of Civil Code No. 40 of 1951, and a threat felony under Article 430 of the Penal Code. Effect: A waiver or signature obtained under threat is invalid and legally ineffective if coercion is established. Action: Do not sign or waive. Document the threat and approach the Investigation Court judge and the judge handling the debt case immediately to establish coercion, seek protection and record the incident officially.

246A former partner or spouse threatens to disclose details of our relationship. What is needed to assess the threat and protect privacy?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Violation of private life under Article 438 and threats involving dishonourable allegations under Article 430 or 432 of the Penal Code. For the lawyer: Copies of conversations and voice messages threatening disclosure, proof of the former relationship and when it ended, to seek an immediate summons, a strict judicial non-interference undertaking, and seizure and forensic examination of phones and devices to remove the private material.

247Someone made fabricated or AI-generated images or recordings and threatens publication. Which original materials should I prepare?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Blackmail and digital forgery under Article 430 read with Articles 295 and 438 of the Penal Code. Examination materials: Your original unedited images, the fabricated media in its original digital file format and the threatening correspondence. Technical route: The lawyer asks the Investigation Court judge to refer the files to the Criminal Evidence Directorate's voiceprint and digital-media section to establish AI fabrication and technical falsification and obtain an arrest warrant.

248I paid a blackmailer, but they demand more and threaten me again. What should a lawyer know about previous transfers and agreements?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Practical court position: A blackmailer is never satisfied. The previous payment is not an argument against him; rather, it is the strongest material evidence of his guilt, establishing the financial-blackmail element. For the lawyer: Earlier transfer receipts from Zain Cash, exchange offices or banks, dates and recipient names, and WhatsApp conversations demanding more, to connect the transfers to blackmail, seize his funds and require repayment of the money taken.

249I live abroad and face threats or blackmail from someone in Iraq. What is needed to assess remote follow-up?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Classification and jurisdiction: Iraqi courts have exclusive territorial jurisdiction because the offender carries out the criminal activity within Iraq under Article 6 of the Penal Code. Required: Execute a barcoded consular litigation power of attorney at the Iraqi embassy in your country of residence and provide digital evidence, including screenshots, recordings, numbers and accounts. The lawyer registers the complaint with the Karkh or Rusafa Investigation Court and pursues the suspect through the National Security Service.

250Someone repeatedly follows me near home or work without assaulting me. Which facts help assess the situation?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Public harassment, disturbance and indecent conduct under Article 402 of the Penal Code, or an attempted offence under Article 31. For the lawyer: A schedule of dates and times, the vehicle's registration, model and colour, street and workplace CCTV, and testimony from guards or neighbours, to obtain a formal summons and a non-interference undertaking backed by security.

251I found a suspected tracker in my car or belongings. What do the lawyer and examiner need?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Violation of another's property and unlawful surveillance under Article 438 of the Penal Code and Article 40 of the Constitution. Immediate action: Do not tamper with the device or remove its SIM card yourself, to avoid destroying fingerprints. Approach the Investigation Court judge immediately to give a statement and refer the vehicle and device for forensic fingerprint examination. Contact the telecommunications company to identify the SIM owner and trace GPS activity to identify the offender promptly.

252After separation, my former partner monitors my movements and waits where I go. What details should I organise?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Unlawful following and interference with personal liberty under Articles 402 and 438 of the Penal Code. Required: Photographs and videos documenting appearances, witnesses at repeatedly visited locations and messages demonstrating knowledge of your whereabouts, to bring a criminal complaint and seek protection and an undertaking not to approach within a specified geographical area.

253Someone keeps contacting me from new accounts and numbers despite my refusal. What needs reviewing for a complaint?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Misuse of communications devices, harassment, disturbance and interference with public tranquillity under Article 402 of the Penal Code and telecommunications instructions. Required: Repeated-call records, blocked-number lists and messages expressly asking the person to stop. The Investigation Court judge contacts telecommunications companies to link the numbers to the respondent and refer the matter for trial.

254I receive unwanted sexual messages or images from a known person or unknown account. Which evidence is needed?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Indecent conduct, offences against public morals and electronic sexual harassment under Articles 400 and 402 of the Penal Code. Evidence: Screenshots of the messages and explicit images with dates and profile links. Do not delete the chat. Approach the Investigation Court to arrange forensic extraction from the phone and immediate issuance of an arrest warrant or summons.

255Someone photographs me or my car, tracks my locations and sends me the pictures. What helps assess threats or stalking?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Violation of private life under Article 438, coupled with an implied threat under Article 432 or 430 of the Penal Code. Required: Keep the images as received, extract metadata if possible, capture the conversation and present it as tangible evidence of intimidation and criminal following to seek an arrest warrant against the photographer.

256I think a neighbour's camera records inside my home or private space. Which information and images are needed?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Voyeurism and violation of the home and private life under Article 438 read with Article 428 of the Penal Code. For the lawyer: Clear photographs of the camera angle showing that it overlooks the internal courtyard or rooms rather than the public street, together with title or lease documents. Procedure: Seek an urgent inspection and evidence-preservation order from the Investigation Court judge for a technical team to examine the field of view, require the neighbour to change the angle and seize recordings intruding on privacy.

257I suspect someone tracks my location through a phone or shared account without my current consent. What technical details need review?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Unauthorised access to information systems and electronic surveillance under Article 438 of the Penal Code and Electronic Transactions Law No. 78 of 2012. Examination: Review devices connected to the Apple ID or Google Account, examine Family Sharing, Find My and Google Maps Location Sharing, and submit the phone for forensic examination documenting the intrusion and surveillance before revoking access, for use in court.
