258I delayed reporting an assault. Which dates, reports and evidence should I prepare?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Classification and periods: Assault under Article 413 of the Penal Code or permanent disability under Article 412. For minor injury, the complaint right expires three months after learning of the offence under Article 3 of the Criminal Procedure Law; this period does not extinguish felony proceedings. Immediate requirements: Attend a public hospital to record bruises or fractures and obtain an official medical report. Document the delay's reason, such as an unavoidable impediment or attempted tribal settlement, identify eyewitnesses and request formal forensic-medical referral to assess incapacity, injury type and timing.

259Someone brandished a weapon or tool and threatened me, but caused no injury. Which details affect the legal assessment?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Strict legal classification: Brandishing a firearm or bladed weapon is an aggravating circumstance. If accompanied by a demand, instruction or requirement to refrain from an act, it is a felony under Article 430(First) of the Penal Code, punishable by imprisonment for up to seven years or detention. A bare threat without a demand is a misdemeanour under Article 432, alongside unlicensed weapon possession under Weapons Law No. 51 of 2017. For the lawyer: The weapon type, such as a pistol, Kalashnikov or knife, CCTV and witnesses present, to seek an arrest warrant and confiscation of the unlicensed weapon.

260Someone tried to force entry into my home or shop, then withdrew. Which evidence helps assess a complaint?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: A felony or misdemeanour involving violation of another's property and attempted entry into a dwelling under Article 428 read with Article 31 of the Penal Code. Required: Nearby CCTV, a police site inspection recording breakage or attempts to open doors or windows, and neighbours' testimony, to establish the intention to break in and seek an arrest warrant.

261My car or property was damaged after a dispute or threat. Which evidence links the damage without assuming the offender's identity?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Intentional destruction or damage to movable property under Article 477 of the Penal Code. Linking the offender without a malicious accusation: Complain against an unknown offender while identifying a person suspected because of earlier threats. Provide the threats as motive and judicial evidence, review shop and street CCTV for movements and timing, and obtain a police inspection recording damage to support civil and criminal compensation claims.

262Several people fought, and each side accuses the other of assault. What does a lawyer need to establish my role?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Fighting and mutual assault under Article 413 or 415 of the Penal Code. Defence materials: Full video from the beginning to identify the initial aggressor and defender, medical reports for all parties showing injury severity, and independent neutral witnesses rather than relatives, to establish the absence of aggressive intent and a lawful self-defence argument against the charge.

263A manager, colleague or customer assaulted me at work. Which facts about the incident and employment require review?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Assault in a public or work setting, a violation of Labour Law No. 37 of 2015 and Articles 413 and 434 of the Penal Code. Required: Workplace CCTV, a written HR incident report and witnesses among colleagues. Bring a complaint before the Investigation Court judge and, in parallel, a Labour Court claim for compensation and termination of the abusive contract where the employer is the assailant.

264I am accused of assault but say I defended myself or another person. Which sequence and evidence does defence counsel need?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal and constitutional classification: Rely on lawful self-defence under Article 42 of the Penal Code, excluding criminal and civil liability. The lawyer must establish: A serious, imminent, unlawful danger directed at you or another person. Inability to obtain protection from public authorities at that moment. A proportionate defensive act without excess. Evidence: Video from the beginning, your medical report showing you were struck first, and witnesses establishing a reactive act to repel a deadly attack.

265My child was assaulted at school or in a public place. Which information and reports are needed?
Answer as provided by the lawyer

Legal classification: Assault on a minor, violation of Juvenile Welfare Law No. 76 of 1983 and Articles 413 and 415 of the Penal Code, and the school's and education administration's tort liability. Required: The child's forensic medical report, the school investigation and discipline-board report, and teacher and pupil statements. Bring a criminal complaint, referring a juvenile offender to the Juvenile Court, and a civil damages action before the Court of First Instance against the offender's guardian and school administration for failure of supervision and protection.
